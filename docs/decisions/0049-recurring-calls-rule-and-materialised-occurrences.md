# 0049 Recurring calls: a stored rule, occurrences created ahead as items, one Teams event per call

Date: 2026-10-19
Status: accepted

## Context
Of the UX review's "recurring tasks and templates", the owner decided that only calls repeat (owner decision of 2026-10-18). A call is the knowledge `meeting` item, extended by Teams T1 ([ADR 0036](0036-teams-graph-app-only-plain-fetch.md)). A call must repeat weekly, every two weeks, or monthly on the same date or weekday, with an optional end date or count. Editing must offer "this call" and "this and the following"; one call can be cancelled; reminders, transcription and recording stay per call; with Teams configured, the calls get Teams meetings. The two shapes considered: **A**, store the rule and let a pg-boss job create the next occurrences; **B**, a series id with every occurrence created up front.

## Decision
- **A with B's rows: a rule on a series, occurrences created ahead as real call items.** `knowledge_meeting_series` (knowledge module, migration 0062) holds the rule (`frequency`, the creator's IANA `time_zone`, an anchor occurrence, `until` or `count`), the template of the next calls (title, notes, visibility, folder, meeting link, reminder offsets and added recipients) and the cursor `next_index` / `next_starts_at`. Each call is an ordinary `meeting` item with `knowledge_meetings.series_id` and `series_index` (unique together). The calls inside a 60-day window are created at once in the create's transaction; the hourly job `knowledge.meeting-series` (stately) fills the window as it moves, through the partial index on `next_starts_at`.
  - Why not B alone: an open-ended series ("every Monday") has no last occurrence to create, and a two-year weekly series would put 100 rows into every list and the dashboard at once.
  - Why not A without rows (virtual occurrences): transcripts, recordings, AI summaries, tasks from the call, reminders, Teams links, search, the list and the dashboard all hang off a knowledge item. Real items reuse all of them unchanged, with the same permission checks.
- **Dates in the creator's time zone.** The local time of day stays across daylight saving time; monthly on the 31st falls on a shorter month's last day, monthly on the fifth weekday on the month's last such weekday. The math lives in `@spolu/shared` (`meeting-series.ts`), used by the server and by the form's labels. No date library: `Intl` gives the zone's wall time.
- **"This call" is the normal edit.** "This and following" (`seriesScope: 'following'`) applies the edit's title, notes, visibility, folder, meeting link, start and reminder to the later calls and to the template, in the same transaction and through the same `applyUpdate` as the call's own edit (so `lockForWrite`, `canEditItem` and the visibility rules run per call); a moved start re-anchors the rule at the edited call. Transcript, recording, summary and linked items stay per call. The later calls' edits notify nobody (one "rescheduled" for the edited call).
- **Cancelling is deleting.** A deleted call is never created again: `next_index` only moves forward. "Delete this and following" deletes the later calls with the same checks and ends the series (`count` = the call's index); from the first call it removes the series.
- **Reminders per call.** The create form's reminder times become offsets from the start; each new call gets its own reminder set at those offsets (only times still ahead), its author the series' author, its added recipients those still pickable.
- **Teams: one Outlook event per call, not a recurring Graph event.** "Create Teams meeting" with "Also for the following meetings" stores a plan (`teams_series_plans`, teams module); the job `teams.series` (every 5 minutes, and once right after the create) creates an event for each later call from the plan's `next_index` that has none and has not started, as the plan's organizer. Each call then has its own online meeting, join link, transcript window, recording switch and public page, exactly as a single call, and the existing transcript poll works untouched. A recurring Graph event would share one online meeting and join link across calls (T1 allows one call per online meeting and windows transcripts by time), need exception handling for moved and cancelled calls, and fail as a whole on one bad occurrence. The cost: invitees get one invitation per call instead of one series invitation. A fixed `transactionId` and nonce per call make a retried create converge on one event; the plan's cursor makes a disconnected call stay disconnected.
- **Permissions as for single calls.** Creating a series needs what creating a call needs (`canReadGroup`, `canSetVisibility`); every later call is checked like its own edit or delete; the Teams plan is admins only (`canPlanTeamsSeries` in `modules/teams/policy.ts`).

## Consequences
- No new dependency. Two new tables, two columns, two jobs.
- A call further out than 60 days does not exist yet; lists, search and the calendar export see only created calls.
- Editing a call's time does not move its Outlook event (as in T1); "Delete" leaves a created Outlook event in place (cancelling it is T2).
- Linked knowledge items of the first call are not copied to later calls; the reminders' later recipients are the template's.
