# Direct messages (DM)

One-to-one private chats. Admins can write to anyone. Clients can write to admins and to people in their own organization (group) only, and cannot see users of other organizations.

## Requirements

- An **administrator** can write to anyone.
- A **client** can write to administrators and to people **from their own organization (group)**.
- A client does **not see** users of other organizations and cannot write to them.
- Realtime, attachments (files, images).
- Unread indicators and notifications (see `notifications.md`).
