# Finance (Financie)

Phase 32. The owner's request, the decisions and the design are in the [spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-finance-module-design.md); the five pull requests in the [plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-17-finance-module.md). The UI calls it **Financie** (tab, header link) and **Finančná správa** (EN "Financial administration", the page title). This file describes what is built; each part fills its own section.

## Access

Built in part A1.

- **The finance right.** Only an admin can hold it (a **finance admin**); the superadmin always has it without a grant. Clients never (a database CHECK on `user.finance_access_at`).
- **Who grants it.** Only the superadmin, on the Admins page: every admin's row has a **Finance** switch (the superadmin's own row has none; other admins see no switches and `GET /api/admins` shows them nobody's right). The switch acts at once (`PUT` / `DELETE /api/admins/:id/finance-access`) and shows a toast ("Finance access granted: <name>." / "… removed …") with **Undo** (sk "Vrátiť späť", 5 s), which switches it back through the same endpoints. A finance admin cannot pass it on.
- **Live.** A grant or a removal acts on the admin's next request (sessions read the user row every time) and on their open app at once: their sockets join or leave the `finance` room and get `me:changed`, so the **Financie** tab and header link appear or vanish without a reload, and an open Finance screen turns into the not-found screen.
- **Deactivation** removes the right; an admin added back needs a new grant.
- **Where it shows.** For finance admins only: the **Financie** tab, the last pill of every client (`/groups/<id>/finance`); the **Financie** header link after Knowledge (`/finance`), a row of the section menu under 1280 px. Anyone else gets the not-found screen at those addresses, and `403` from every finance route.
- **The screens (empty until parts B and C).** The client tab is a grid of three cards: **Overview** (top left, 70 % of the width), **Documents** (bottom left), **Work log** (the whole right column, 30 %). From 1024 px the grid is at least the window's height under the group header; Overview is as tall as its charts (never scrolled inside, owner request 2026-10-18), Documents takes the rest (at least 360 px) and Documents and Work log scroll inside themselves; from 641 to 1023 px Overview spans the top and Documents and Work log sit side by side; on phones a segmented control **Overview · Documents · Work log** (remembered per person) shows one card at a time. `/finance` has the same grid across clients (Overview, **Contracts**, Work log) with the two ranking cards under it (a fourth segment **Rankings** on phones). Until the parts land, each card says in one line what it will hold.
- **Sealed.** Finance data never reaches anyone without the right: finance rows live in their own tables (no knowledge list, search, chat picker or digest reads them, [ADR 0027](../decisions/0027-sealed-finance-module.md)); finance files are readable only by finance admins and can be attached by no other module; finance events go to the `finance` room only; the **Finance** notification group shows in Settings only to finance admins and its events reach only people who hold the right when they are delivered; task and ticket answers carry no time and no money (their time has its own routes, answering only about the caller unless they are a finance admin).

## Time on done

Built in part A2 (spec §5 and §5.6).

- **When.** A task is completed (`completed_at` goes from empty to a time: the done circle, a drag into Completed, the panel's bucket select, also on the global board, or creating it straight in Completed), or a ticket's status becomes **Vyriešený** (resolved). Moving inside Completed is no new completion.
- **Who is asked.** Every active admin (and the superadmin) among the task's assignees or the ticket's riešitelia, whether or not they hold the finance right; clients never. The admin who completed it gets the dialog at once; the others get the notification **Zapíšte strávený čas** (kind `task.time_missing` in the Task board group, `ticket.time_missing` in the Ticket board group, passing every mode, once) and a **Zapíšte strávený čas** row on the dashboard's My tasks card until they answer (0 is an answer). An admin who is no assignee, or a client, completing the task gets no dialog; every admin assignee is notified.
- **One entry per admin per task (ticket).** It counts on the Bratislava day of the first completion (resolution). Reopening drops the unanswered requests, filled time stays; completing again asks only those with no filled time. Removing an assignee (riešiteľ) drops their request, not their filled time. Deleting a task drops the requests; filled entries stay with the task's title ("deleted task"). Time filled before the completion (in the panel) starts counting at the completion, and that admin is not asked.
- **The dialog.** "Koľko času ste na tom strávili?" with the task's title and client; a field that reads `1:30`, `1,5`, `90m`, `2h 15m` (shown back as "1 h 30 min"), chips 15 min, 30 min, 1 h, 2 h, 4 h and **Žiadny čas** (0); above 12 h "To je viac ako pracovný deň. Je to správne?", above 100 h refused. **Uložiť** saves, **Neskôr** closes and leaves the request (the dashboard row, no notification). Several completions open one dialog after another. A full-screen sheet on phones. Its own lazy chunk.
- **Tickets.** "Koľko času ste strávili na #123 Predmet?". When a linked task (made from the ticket) has logged time, two choices: **Čas na tiket** (added to the tasks' hours) or **Hodiny na súvisiacich úlohách to pokrývajú** (stored as 0 minutes, covered by the tasks, so nothing is added and no request stays), with the caller's own minutes on each linked task (never anyone else's).
- **My time.** In the task panel under the assignees, and on the ticket page under the riešitelia: "Nevyplnený" with an amber dot and **Vyplniť**, or the minutes with **Upraviť** (on a ticket also "Pokryté úlohami"); it opens the same dialog, before completion too. A finance admin also sees everyone's time in one line ("Čas: Eva 1 h 30 min, Ján Nevyplnený"). Nobody else sees any of it; the task and ticket answers carry no time.
- **Links.** The push and the email open the task panel or the ticket page with `?time=1`, which opens the dialog for an admin assignee (riešiteľ).
- **Tickets without a client** (owner decision 24, 2026-10-17): asked like any ticket; the entries have no client (`group_id` null, migration 0035), appear in no client's Financie tab, only on `/finance` as **Bez klienta** (part D), and move to the client the ticket gets.

## Documents and contracts

Built in part B (spec §6.1). The client tab's bottom-left card keeps the client's contracts, documents and notes, in folders, like the knowledge base in a smaller frame.

- **Four kinds.** "+ New" offers **Contract** (title, optional number up to 60 characters, an amount with its currency, a period from–to, a description, files, reminders), **Document** (title, description, files), **Note** (title, text, optional files) and **Folder**. Up to 20 files per item; a contract may have none and its row then says "No file" (decision 13).
- **Money.** The amount is typed as people write it (`12 000`, `1200,50`, `1.200,50`; `parseMoney`) and stored as integer cents with its currency. Only **EUR** can be entered now (`ENABLED_CURRENCIES`); the currency select appears once a second currency is enabled. The server refuses a contract without an amount or period, a period that ends before it starts, a disabled currency, and contract fields on a document or a note (`400`).
- **The list.** Breadcrumbs, the open folder's folders first, then its items newest first, 30 a page with "Show more". The type filter (All · Contracts · Documents · Notes; remembered per person) and the search (from 2 characters, without diacritics, over title, number, description and file names) list every folder flat, each result naming its folder path. A contract row shows its number, title, period ("1. 1. 2026 – 31. 12. 2026"), the amount on the right, a bell when a reminder time is ahead, **Ending soon** (the end within 60 days) or **Ended**, and "No file".
- **The item sheet.** Opening an item shows it in a sheet (also from a link, `?item=<id>`, which notifications and the dashboard use): the amount, period and number, the description, the files, and for a contract its reminders. Edit in place (a stale edit answers "Someone changed this item meanwhile" with "Load their version"), **Move to…**, **Delete** (with its files and reminders). Folders: rename, move (never into themselves), delete (the contents move one level up).
- **Reminders.** A new contract's form proposes one reminder **30 days before the end at 09:00** (it follows the end date until edited; none when that moment has passed), saved with the contract; its author gets it. Afterwards the sheet edits the times and the recipients on their own, as on a task. **Recipients can only be finance admins**: the picker lists them only and the server refuses anyone else; a recipient who loses the right gets nothing when a time fires. The notification is in the **Finance** group ("Contract ends on 31 Dec 2026"; the digest line "Contract <title> · <client>: ends on …", button "Open contract"), never with the amount, and opens the item. The upcoming reminders on the admin home list it for its recipients only. The calendar export works as everywhere.
- **Files** are uploaded as finance files from the start: readable by finance admins only, attachable to finance items only.
- **Live.** Every change refreshes the card of the other finance admins who have the client open (`finance:changed`, kind `items`).
- **Never elsewhere.** No contract, document, note or finance file shows in the knowledge tab, the global knowledge list or search, the chat's knowledge picker or the "Open in Claude" prompt (tests in `modules/finance/documents-leaks.test.ts`).

## Contracts: master, payment schedule, linked documents

Owner request of 2026-10-21 ([ADR 0066](../decisions/0066-contract-masters-schedules-and-payment-links.md)). Finance admins only, like every finance item.

- **Amount of 0.** A contract (of one client or several), its shares, a schedule's payment and a document's payment may all be 0.
- **Master contract (Rámcová zmluva).** A contract's form has an optional **Rámcová zmluva** select: the client's contracts and the contracts of several clients that include it (`GET /api/finance/contract-choices?group=`; on `/finance` a contract of several clients chooses among those of several clients). **One level only:** a contract under a master cannot be a master (it is not offered), and a contract that has contracts under it gets no master (the form says how many are under it). **Client scope (decision C3):** every client of a contract under a master is a client of the master — a client's contract under the client's own master or under a contract of several clients that includes it; a contract of several clients only under one whose clients include all of its clients; the master's shares cannot drop such a client. The server refuses anything else (`400 invalid_parent`, the form says why). The sheet shows the master on the contract and **Zmluvy pod rámcovou** on the master (in a client's tab only those the client may see); each opens its item. Deleting a master leaves its contracts without one (each records it).
- **Payment schedule (Platobný kalendár).** None, **every month**, **every quarter**, **every 6 months**, **every year**, **every N months** (1–120) or **on chosen days** (up to 120), with the amount per payment, the first payment and an optional last day (empty: until the contract ends). Due days keep the first day's day of the month and fall on a shorter month's last day (31 January → 28 or 29 February → 31 March). A schedule with no payment before the contract ends, or more than 600, is refused (`400 invalid_schedule`), also when the contract's end moves before it. **Nothing is created from a schedule.**
- **Expected payments (očakávané).** The contract's sheet shows the schedule in one line ("+600 € každý mesiac"), **Očakávané doteraz**, **Prijaté**, **Ešte očakávané**, the next expected day and the periods around today (all with **Zobraziť všetky**), each marked **Uhradené**, **Čiastočne uhradené**, **Neuhradené** or **Očakávané** (a filled, half, empty or dashed square with its word). A period runs from its due day to the next one (the last stays open, so a late payment counts); it is paid when the payments of the linked documents dated in it add up to its amount. In a client's tab a contract of several clients counts the client's part (its share of the whole, whole cents).
- **In the Summary (Súhrn).** The Money view adds the total **Očakávané platby (EUR)** of the period with "prijaté …" under it, and under the chart a section **Očakávané platby**: a line per scheduled contract of the client (on `/finance`, of the chosen clients) with its title, "+600 € každý mesiac", a mark for each of the latest six due periods, how many are unpaid and the next day; in a client's tab a line opens the contract. They are counted apart from the contracts' money (which still counts the whole amount on the start day).
- **Linked documents.** A **Dokument**'s form has an optional **Zmluva** (a contract of its client, or for the organisation's document a contract of several clients; `400 invalid_contract` otherwise) and, with one, an optional **Prijatá platba** with its **Dátum platby** (both or neither). The document's sheet shows its contract; the contract's sheet lists **Prepojené dokumenty** with their payments. One contract per document. These links are apart from the documents' links to salaries and expenses ([Salaries](#salaries-mzdy)).
- **Files.** A contract keeps its own finance-only files as before (up to 20).
- **MCP.** `get_contract`, `get_contract_payments`, `list_contract_attachments`, `list_contract_choices`, `link_finance_document_to_contract`; `create_finance_document`, `create_organisation_finance_item` and `update_finance_document` take `parentId`, `schedule` and `contractLink` ([mcp.md](../integrations/mcp.md)); finance holders only.
- **History.** A contract's master, schedule (one line), contracts under it and documents, a document's contract, payment and its day are fields of the item's rows; a link or a master change records a row on both items.
- **Tests.** `modules/finance/contracts.test.ts` (zero, the one-level and client rules, schedules, paid detection, the overview, choices, the right, audit), `packages/shared/src/finance/contracts.test.ts` (the schedule maths: month ends, leap years, custom days, parts of shares), `modules/mcp/leaks.test.ts`, web `documents/contract-form.test.ts` and `item-sheet.test.tsx`, e2e `e2e/finance-contracts.spec.ts`.

## Overview and work log

Built in part C (spec §6.1–6.3).

- **Overview card** (top left; shown as "Summary", sk "Súhrn", so that "Prehľad" stays the group tab). A **Money · Hours** switch and a period filter (last 12 and next 12 months by default, this year, last year, all time, a custom month range), both remembered per person, the custom range and the chosen currency too.
  - **Money:** a bar chart by month — the contracts **paid** (start date today or earlier in Bratislava, solid ink) stacked under those **to be paid** (later start, hatched), and next to them a thinner blue bar of what the work **cost** that month (hours × rates). On the right the totals per currency: **Paid**, **To be paid**, **Spent on work** (EUR) and **Balance** (EUR paid − spent, green or red). Currencies are never added together: with UAH contracts a currency switch appears and the cost bar shows only for EUR. "N h without an hourly rate" opens the rates dialog.
- **Filters in the URL** (2026-10-18). The client filter and the page period are URL params: `clients=<id,id>` (absent: every client), `internal=0` (every client without eGrant tím), `period=lastYear|thisMonth|lastMonth|next12|allTime|custom` (absent: this year) and, for `custom`, `from` and `to` (`YYYY-MM`). Each change replaces the history entry. A link or a saved view opens the same filters; a visit with none of them gets the person's last ones (`spolu.filters.<userId>.finance.page`), written into the URL, and default filters forget them. The filters remembered before the move (`finance.clients`, `finance.page.period`, `.custom`) are moved into that entry once. `?item=` (an organisation item's link) is kept and is no filter. The cards' own choices (Money · Hours, the ledger's type and sort, the Položky view, the rankings' admin and currency, a card's project) stay remembered in the browser only.
- **Saved views** under the filters (finance admins only; see [admin-home.md](admin-home.md)): the starter **Tento mesiac** (`?period=thisMonth`), then the person's own.
  - **Hours:** the total line (hours · tasks · tickets · cost), then one small chart per admin who worked for the client in the period, all on one scale, ordered by hours, each with the admin's hours and cost; the first 6, then "Show all (n)".
  - Every chart has **Show as table**; each month is a focusable column with a tooltip. The charts are our own SVG ([ADR 0028](../decisions/0028-finance-charts-own-svg.md)).
- **Work log card** (right). On top the period filter (all time by default) and the summary: total hours · tasks · tickets · cost and a line per admin ("Eva 12 h 30 min · 8 tasks · 625 €"; collapsed to one line on phones). Below, the chronology **oldest at the top, newest at the bottom**, opening scrolled to the bottom; **Show earlier** loads the previous 200 entries and keeps the scroll position. Each month starts with its full totals (also when only part of the month is on the page) and, with more than one admin, a line per admin; then the days, each entry with the admin, the task (a link to its panel), the ticket or the manual note, the duration and its cost. Requests not answered yet show as **Waiting for time** and count in no total. A ticket and the tasks made from it are **one unit**: the ticket's row (#number and live subject, a link to the ticket) with its own entries and the linked tasks indented under it, on the day of the ticket's entry (or the unit's first entry that month); a ticket answered "covered by the tasks" shows that and adds 0. Every total sums each row once. A deleted task or ticket keeps its stored title, marked "deleted".
- **Entry menu.** Edit (a manual entry: day, duration, note; a task or ticket entry: the duration only), **Record time** for a request, **Reset to 0** for a filled task or ticket entry (never deleted: the work was done), **Delete** for a manual entry or a request, confirmed inline under the row ("Delete this entry?", destructive Delete and Cancel; never `window.confirm`).
- **Add time** (manual entry, spec §6.3): admin (every active admin, and a removed one who has a rate), day (today by default, never in the future), duration (`1:30`, `1,5`, `90m`, `2h 15m`; 0 allowed), note (up to 500 characters). Finance admins only.
- **Hourly rates** (from the work log's header on a client's tab; on `/finance` only from the page's **Hodinové sadzby** beside its title): every admin with the rate that applies today and **Change**; a new rate applies from the day chosen (default the first of this month; an admin's first rate **from the start**, which covers all their earlier work); entering a rate for a day that already has one corrects it. The history per admin shows each row's day, rate and who set it, with Delete for a mistaken row, confirmed inline in that row. Rates are EUR. An entry's cost uses the rate of its day; a past cost changes only when someone changes a past rate.
- **Cost rounding.** Each shown total is `round(sum(minutes × rate) / 60)` cents, rounded once per total, never per entry; minutes without an applicable rate count in the hours but not in the cost, and the totals say how many.
- **Live.** Every write (entry, rate) pushes `finance:changed` to the finance admins; the open cards refetch.
- **Entries without a client** (owner decision 24, time on a ticket that has no client, part A2; or a manual entry added on `/finance` with **Bez klienta**) never appear in a client's overview or work log; only the system-wide page (part D) shows them.

## Finance by project and event

Built in Phase 33 part P6 ([spec §11](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-phase-33-projects-design.md), decisions 23–25).

- **Filters on a client's tab.** The Overview and the Work log each have a **Project** pill (any, **No project**, or one of the client's projects, archived ones last) and, with a project chosen, an **Event** pill (any, **No event**, or one of its events, coming ones first). Each card remembers its choice per person and per client. Hours and cost count the entries of that project or event; the money counts the contracts of that project (**No project**: the contracts that name none, the client's shares of contracts of several clients too). Under an event filter the money view shows no contract money and says "Contracts are not tied to events": only the cost of the event's work shows.
- **A ticket unit splits by project.** Every entry carries its own project: a ticket's entries follow the ticket, a task's the task, so a task made from a ticket but set on another project drops out of the ticket's unit under the ticket's project and shows under its own.
- **`/finance`.** The Project pill appears on the Overview and the Work log only while exactly one client is chosen (decision 24); there is no Event pill there, and the rankings and the ledger take no project. The admin group **eGrant tím** does not count as a client here (it is ticked by default, Phase 34 part E, below): one client with eGrant tím ticked still shows the pill, and a chosen project narrows both cards to that client, so the team's time (which belongs to no client's project) and the organisation's expenses drop out while it is set (decision of 2026-10-18 at the rebase of P6 onto P34-E). The server answers `400 invalid_filter` to a project with no or several clients (eGrant tím alone counts as none), to any event on `/finance`, and to an event without a project. On the admin group's own tab a Project or Event filter leaves the organisation's expenses out.
- **Contract.** The contract form of one client has an optional **Project** select (the client's active projects, the current one when archived); a contract of several clients, a document or a note has none (`400`). A project of another client answers `400 invalid_project`.
- **Manual entry.** Add time and the edit of a manual entry offer the client's **Project** and, with one chosen, **Event**; an entry without a client has none. A task or ticket entry takes neither: it follows its task or ticket.
- **How the copy stays right.** When a task is completed or a ticket resolved, its entries (requested and filled) copy the project and event; the own time a riešiteľ fills copies them too; every later change of the task's or ticket's project or event moves all its entries in the same transaction (and tells the finance room). A ticket moved to another client clears its entries' copies (projects are per client). A merge moves the merged project's entries and contracts to the survivor (part P3's merge runs the hook). A deleted project or event leaves its ids on the entries and contracts: the pills no longer offer it, so its time shows under **Any** only. Entries made before migration 0045 get their copies once from the startup job `finance.backfill-projects`.
- **Never elsewhere.** The projects module's answers (a project, its events, the timeline, the cards) carry no money, hours or contract, even for a finance admin (`modules/finance/projects-leaks.test.ts`); a project change that moves entries reaches the finance room only.
- **Tests.** `modules/finance/projects.test.ts` (copies, filters, the one-client rule, contracts, manual entries, `403`), `seal.test.ts`, `backfill-projects.test.ts`, `projects-leaks.test.ts`; end to end `e2e/finance-projects.spec.ts` (spec §14 scenario 9).

## System-wide

Built in part D (spec §6.2), at `/finance` (header link **Financie**, finance admins only; anyone else gets "Page not found").

- **Filters.** On top the **client filter** of the knowledge list (a pill with a popover, a sheet on phones, chips of the chosen clients): every client by default; choosing all of them, none, or **Predvolené** means every client, also those added later. Next to it **one period for every card** (this year by default; last year, this or last month, the last 12 and next 12 months, all time, a custom range): it drives the Overview, the Contracts table (the contracts running in the period; a search ignores it), the Work log and both rankings, which have no period of their own here (with exactly one client chosen the Overview and the Work log add a Project pill, Phase 33 P6; never an event, never on the rankings). **Hodinové sadzby** (top right, beside the title; the only rates button on the page, the work log card has none here) opens the rates dialog.
- **The same grid across the chosen clients.** **Overview** (money and cost of the chosen clients; in the hours view one chart per admin across them) and **Work log** (the chronology across clients: each entry names its client, each month header adds "Most hours:" with the top three clients). Its **Pridať čas** opens the manual-entry dialog with a **client picker** first: every client, or **Bez klienta** (time that belongs to no client, shown on `/finance` only, migration 0041); only finance admins may add it (the server checks the finance policy, the client must exist). With every client chosen, both also count the **time without a client** (owner decision 24: time on a ticket that has no client), shown as **Bez klienta**; it is in no client's tab.
- **Položky** (Phase 32 E, see [Organisation level](#organisation-level); before it **Contracts**, owner decision 16, in place of the old Phases 14–15): the ledger view lists the chosen clients' contracts as a table — client (logo and name), number and title, period with **Ending soon** (the end within 60 days) and **Ended**, amount — sorted by newest start, soonest end or largest amount, 30 a page with **Show more**. The search (from 2 characters) covers every finance contract, document and note of those clients, a document or note marked as such. A row opens the item in its client's Financie tab. On phones each row stacks into lines.
- **Clients by money paid.** The page period (all time: every contract up to today); the amounts of the contracts that **started** in the period and no later than today (owner decision 12), one currency at a time (a currency switch appears once UAH is enabled, decision 11; the choice remembered per person). Rows: rank, logo, client, amount, a bar, the share of the total; the total above. Equal amounts share a rank.
- **Clients by hours.** The page period, **all admins** or one, and **Hodiny · Peniaze** (the admin and the sort remembered per person): rank by the counted hours or by their cost (hours × rates). Rows: rank, client, hours and cost, "N h without a rate" when some minutes have none (they cost nothing in the Money ranking). With every client chosen the time without a client ranks as one row, **Bez klienta**.
- Both rankings show 20 rows and **Show 20 more**; from 641 px they sit side by side under the grid, on phones they are the fourth segment **Rebríčky**.
- **Live.** Every finance write refreshes the open page (`finance:changed`, 2 s debounce).

## Organisation level

Built in part E (owner request of 2026-10-18; [spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-finance-org-expenses-design.md) with decisions O1–O7 and E1–E22, [ADR 0032](../decisions/0032-finance-items-without-a-client.md)). Items of the organisation have no client and live on `/finance` only (decision O6).

- **Položky.** `/finance`'s bottom-left card has two views (remembered per person): **Zoznam** (List), the ledger of the chosen clients' items with a type filter **Zmluvy · Výdavky · Dokumenty · Poznámky · Všetko** (contracts by default), the sorts (type and sort remembered per person), the search and the page period (contracts running in it, expenses dated in it; documents, notes and every kind have none); and **Organizácia**, the organisation's own items in its own folders, like a client's Documents card (breadcrumbs, Move, rename, delete). The organisation's items (expenses, its documents and notes) show in the ledger only with every client chosen, as the time without a client does; a contract of several clients shows when any chosen client has a share. The client filter has no "only my clients" option. A row of a client's item opens it in that client's tab; an organisation item opens here (`/finance?item=`). On phones the segment is **Položky**.
- **+ Nové** (both views): **Zmluva**, **Výdavok**, **Dokument**, **Poznámka** (and **Priečinok** in the Organizácia view). A contract asks **Pre koho**: one client (made as in its tab, in its top level, and opened there after saving) or **Viacero klientov**; a document or a note: the organisation (default) or one client. An expense and a folder always belong to the organisation.
- **Výdavok (expense).** A payment of the organisation: the title says what it is for (a payment for something, a fine), then the amount with its currency, the date (today, past or future; a later date marks it **plánovaný**), the description, files and reminders: the form proposes one **the day before the date at 09:00** (none when passed), finance admins only as recipients, the notification "Expense due on …" without the amount. The amount, date and title can change later. Until 2026-10-21 an expense could also be **Mzda admina**; salaries are now their own records ([Salaries](#salaries-mzdy)), and the server refuses the salary kind and a payee (`400`). Documents can be linked to an expense (its sheet's **Dokumenty**).
- **Contract of several clients.** The contract's fields plus **Nadradená organizácia** (who pays) and **Podiely klientov**: a row per client with its share, **Pridať klienta**, **Rozdeliť rovnomerne** (whole cents, the first rows take the rest) and a line that says how much is still to split, how much too much, or that the shares add up. The shares must add up **exactly** to the amount, in its currency (decision O1): 2–50 clients, each once, none below 0 (0 allowed since 2026-10-21); the form and the server refuse anything else (`400`). On `/finance` the sheet shows the whole amount, the payer and every share (each client links to its tab); the contract is edited there only (decision O2): new shares, amount, payer, period, number, text and files (a new amount needs shares that add up to it). It cannot be turned into a contract of one client or back (delete and create again, decision E9).
- **In a client's tab** a contract of several clients sits at the top level of the Documents card (and in its type filter and search) with **that client's share as its amount** and the line "Nadradená organizácia: …". Its sheet is read-only: the client's share, the whole amount, the payer, period, number, text and files, and **Upraviť vo Financiách**; never the other clients' names or shares (the server answers the tab with its own share only, decision E10), no reminders, no Move or Delete.
- **Money.** A client's Overview counts its shares on the contract's start date; the paid ranking ranks each client by its own contracts plus its shares (decision O7). On `/finance` with every client the Overview adds the organisation's money out (decision O3, E17): a coral bar per month, **Výdavky** paid out (date today or earlier, and the salaries of months that have begun, with "z toho mzdy adminov"), **Plánované výdavky** (later dates and months), and the balance becomes **Bilancia po výdavkoch** = paid − expenses paid out; **Minuté na prácu** stays as information and is not subtracted, because the salaries already pay for that work. With chosen clients, and in every client's tab, nothing of the expenses shows and the balance stays paid − cost of work. Expenses are in no ranking (decision E18).
- **Files and reminders** of organisation items are finance-only like the rest (`POST /api/finance/org/files`); their reminder notifications open `/finance?item=…`.
- **Live.** A write of an organisation item sends `finance:changed` with no client: every open finance screen refetches, each client's tab too.
- **Never elsewhere.** No expense, payee or shared contract reaches the knowledge lists or search, the chat, a client's tab (beyond its own share), a group room or anyone without the finance right (tests in `modules/finance/org-leaks.test.ts` and `org.test.ts`).

## Salaries (Mzdy)

Owner request of 2026-10-21 ([ADR 0065](../decisions/0065-salaries-own-records-and-cost-document-links.md)). A **Mzda** is what the organisation paid one admin for one month: its own record, no longer an expense.

- **Who.** Finance admins only. Anyone else, an admin without the right too, does not see even their own salary: every salary route answers `404` (not `403`), the files open for finance admins only, the MCP tools are finance tools.
- **Fields.** The admin (the active admins and the superadmin, `GET /api/finance/payees`; an old salary keeps an admin removed since), the month (year and month), the amount paid in whole cents (0 or more, EUR), an optional note, up to 20 files (the organisation's finance files, `POST /api/finance/org/files`) and linked documents.
- **Where.** `/finance`'s Položky card has a third view, **Mzdy** (beside Zoznam and Organizácia): the salaries of the page period (its months; all time: every salary), newest month first, 50 a page with **Show more**, an **admin** filter (all admins or one, remembered in the browser like the cards' other choices, so links and saved views of the page keep their filters), and on top the totals of the filter: the whole sum, a line per admin and a line per month. **+ Nová mzda** opens the form; a row opens the salary's sheet (also from a link, `/finance?salary=<id>`): edit (a stale edit answers "Someone changed this meanwhile"), the files, **Dokumenty** (link a finance document, unlink one) and **Delete** (confirmed inline).
- **Money.** On `/finance` with every client (and the admin group's tab) a salary is money out in the Overview, where the salary expenses were: paid out from the first day of its month (with "z toho mzdy adminov"), planned before; it changes no hourly rate and no cost of work (decision O4). Never with chosen clients, never in a client's tab, never in a ranking.
- **Linked documents.** A finance document (a client's or the organisation's, type Dokument) links to a salary or an expense; each document's sheet on `/finance` lists the salaries and expenses it is linked to. A link is made once (again changes nothing); deleting the document, the salary or the expense removes it. These links are apart from the links of documents to contracts.
- **From the expenses.** Migration 0069 made every salary expense with an admin a salary of the same id: the month of its date, the amount, the title and description as the note, the files, who made it and when; both get a `migrated` row in the history. Their reminders went with the expenses (a salary has none).
- **History.** Created, edited (admin, month, amount as money, the note as „zmenený“, files, documents) and deleted; a link of a document is an edit of the salary or of the expense (`documents`).
- **Live.** Every write sends `finance:changed` with no client: every open finance screen refetches.
- **Tests.** `modules/finance/salaries.test.ts` (permissions with the 404, files, filters and totals, money out, links, audit), `salaries-migration.test.ts` (a seeded database upgraded through 0069, the data part run twice, the schema equal to a fresh one), `schema.test.ts`, `policy.test.ts`, `mcp/leaks.test.ts`; web `features/finance/salaries/salaries.test.tsx` and `org.test.tsx`; end to end `e2e/finance-salaries.spec.ts`.

## The admin group "eGrant tím"

Built in Phase 34 part P34-E ([spec §8](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-phase-34-admin-group-design.md#8-finance-in-the-admin-group-part-p34-e-after-phase-32-d), decisions 7 and 13), reconciled with the [organisation level](#organisation-level): the team's money is the organisation's, so the admin group keeps **no finance store of its own**, and its time counts on `/finance` by default.

- **Its Financie tab** (finance admins only, like every Financie tab; an admin without the right sees no pill, the not-found screen at the address and `403` from every route) is the organisation's: the bottom-left card is **Organizácia**, the same items and folders as `/finance`'s Organizácia view (expenses, the organisation's documents, notes and contracts of several clients, with **+ Nové**); the **Overview** is the time on the team's tasks plus the organisation's expenses as money out ("Bilancia po výdavkoch", as on `/finance`); the **Work log** is the time on its tasks (internal time). A change of no client refetches the tab's Organizácia card too.
- **Time is asked** on its tasks like on a client's (decision 13: internal work has a cost).
- **No items of its own.** `GET`/`POST /api/groups/<admin group>/finance/items`, `/folders` and `/files` answer `409 admin_group`; a contract of several clients cannot give it a share (`400 invalid_group`); the share pickers list client groups only.
- **On `/finance`** (owner decision of 2026-10-18: "the team's spending must also be included in /finance, with the possibility to filter it out") the client filter has **eGrant tím** on top in its own **Interné** section, **ticked by default**: "every client" includes the team's time and cost on its tasks next to the organisation's expenses, in the Overview totals, the Work log and the ledger. Unticking it leaves the admin group out (`internal=0`; the organisation's own items and expenses stay in); among chosen clients it is in when ticked (its id in `clients=`). The choice is in the URL and remembered per person with the rest of the filter.
- **Never ranked.** The paid and hours rankings leave the admin group out whatever the filter, because they compare clients; its hours get no "eGrant tím" row in the hours ranking either (decision of 2026-10-18 with the owner's change: they count in the Overview totals and the Work log only).
- Tests: `apps/server/src/modules/finance/admin-group.test.ts` (leaks first) and `scope.test.ts`; web `features/finance/system/client-filter.test.tsx` and `features/groups/group-layout.test.tsx`; e2e `e2e/admin-group-finance.spec.ts` (scenario 7).

## History of changes (audit log, 2026-10-19)

Every finance change is a **finance row** of the audit log „História zmien“ ([audit-log.md](audit-log.md)): only admins with the finance right (and the superadmin) see it; for anyone else it does not exist, through any filter. Recorded, with the author: an item created, edited (title, number, amount as money, period, payer, shares, folder, project, files, an expense's documents; the note only as „zmenený“) and deleted; a salary created, edited and deleted (see [Salaries](#salaries-mzdy)), and the salary expenses migration 0069 moved (`migrated`, on both sides); a folder created, renamed or moved, and deleted; a time entry created, edited (minutes as minutes, day, note) and deleted, also an admin's own `PUT …/time/me` (the first time `created`, then `updated`); a rate added (a rate of the same day replaces the old one, with the amount from and to) and deleted; the finance right granted and removed. An item's row has the client as its group; a **contract of several clients has no group** and its clients as `shares`, so the „Klient“ filter (which matches the group only) does not find it under each client, while the type filter and the item's own „História“ do. The startup backfill of time entries' projects writes one summary row (`_batch` with the count, author `finance.backfill-projects`). The time requests the app makes on completion are not recorded (they are not time yet). A task's or ticket's own rows never carry time or money.
