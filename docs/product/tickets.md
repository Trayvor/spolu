# Technical support (tickets)

Support tickets come from eGrant's API, from clients and from admins. Each ticket belongs to one client (group), found by the client's eGrant instance link, or to no client yet. A ticket has its own chat, riešitelia (assignees) who answer it, and can be turned into tasks with AI. A person who reported a ticket but is not a client of that group gets a narrow **ziadatel** (requester) membership that sees only their own tickets.

Design: [Phase 21 spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-phase-21-tickets-and-requesters-design.md), [ticket lifecycle rework](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-ticket-lifecycle-rework-design.md) (2026-11-04). Phase 21 supersedes Phases 12 and 13.

## Requirements

- **Sources.**
  - eGrant sends a ticket to `POST /api/tickets/intake` with one shared Bearer key (`TICKETS_API_KEY`); the field list is in [integrations](integrations.md). Every field is optional, but a ticket needs a subject, a body or an area. An optional `request_id` makes retries safe: a repeat answers the first ticket and creates nothing.
  - A client (a manager) creates a ticket in one of their groups and is its initiator, or (2026-10-23) creates it **on behalf of another person**: they name the initiator by email, name and optional phone, only in a group they manage. The initiator is then resolved, emailed and reads the ticket exactly as when an admin names them (below). No extra rate limit beyond the general one for writes (owner decision).
  - An admin creates a ticket for any client and names the initiator (email, name, phone), as eGrant does.
  - A ziadatel never creates tickets.
- **Fields**: subject, question area, text, due date, a URL, the instance link as received, the initiator's name, email and phone, the number `#123` (one sequence for all tickets), status, source (`api` or `manual`), who created it, when it was created, changed and resolved. Since 2026-11-04 the grant programme is no longer shown or edited: a ticket belongs to a **project** of its client (below); the `programme` column stays, filled only by the intake, as context for the AI and for `TICKETS_PROJECT_FIELD=programme`. A ticket carries files: uploaded with the new-ticket form, or sent by eGrant as base64 in the intake's `files` (up to 10, stored in the client's files; types sniffed from the bytes, see [integrations](integrations.md)). A ticket without a client keeps the intake's files outside any group (only admins read them) until a client is assigned; then they move to it. (The external `file_links` of the first version were removed: eGrant sends the files themselves.)
- **Client by instance link.** The instance link is compared with the groups' eGrant URLs by host: scheme, a leading `www.`, the path and the case do not matter (`https://WWW.X.egrant.sk/app` matches `https://x.egrant.sk`). No match, or no link: the ticket is saved **without a client**. Only admins see it (global view, "Without client"); an admin assigns a client by hand. Until then there is no requester account and no email.
- **Initiator** (for a ticket with a client, by its email; an existing account is found by the exact address, else by its mailbox: case, spaces and a `+tag` never matter, nor, for Gmail, the dots and `googlemail.com`, preferring an admin; so an admin's address in another spelling never makes a second account):
  - an admin's email: that admin;
  - a client of the group: that client;
  - anyone else (a client of another group, a ziadatel of another client, a new person): gets a **ziadatel** membership of this group; a new person gets an account first (named from the ticket, Slovak);
  - someone an admin removed from this group, or a disabled account: no account is linked, only the name and email are kept, no email is sent.
  - Every reader sees the initiator's name, email and phone; the linked account's id (`requester.userId` in the API) only admins and the initiator themself (2026-10-23, privacy review).
- **"Ticket created" email.** The initiator gets one email per ticket (in their language) with the number, the subject, the first lines of the text and **Open the ticket**. For a žiadateľ (an initiator who is no admin and no client of any group) the button is a one-click sign-in link ([ADR 0022](../decisions/0022-one-click-ticket-sign-in-link.md)): it signs them in without a code and opens `/tickets/<id>` (after asking for their name if it is empty); it works for 7 days, as often as needed, and the email says so and asks not to forward it. An expired link opens the login page with "The link has expired, sign in with your email" and the email filled in. An admin's or a group client's button goes through the code login (`/login?redirect=/tickets/<id>&email=...`), and their email says a code will be sent. It is sent whatever the notification settings, never twice (not on a later move), and not when the initiator created the ticket themselves.
- **Statuses**: New, In progress, Resolved. Only admins change the status and the fields; resolving records the time, reopening clears it. Opening a ticket and finishing it follow [Lifecycle](#lifecycle-finished-by-both-sides-2026-11-04).
- **Assign or move the client** (admins). The initiator is found again for the new client and the "Ticket created" email goes if it never went. Riešitelia who cannot be one in the new client are dropped (below); a ticket left without a riešiteľ gets the new client's account owners. A move clears the "solved on our side" mark. A ticket with tasks cannot move (`409 has_tasks`): its tasks live on that client's board.
- **No per-ticket following** (owner decision 2026-10-17, [ADR 0021](../decisions/0021-ticket-riesitelia-and-watching.md) amendment). There is no Follow button, no followers list and no "Following" filter: who hears about a ticket follows from its riešitelia (below). The `ticket_followers` table was dropped (migration 0033).
- **Riešitelia (assignees).** A ticket has zero or more riešitelia (EN "Assignees", at most 10), like a task's assignees.
  - Who adds and removes them: admins and the clients of the ticket's group; on a ticket without a client admins only. A ziadatel never (not even on their own ticket).
  - Who can be one: any active admin, and any client of the ticket's group. Never a ziadatel, never a client of another group; a ticket without a client takes admins only.
  - A new riešiteľ is notified (`ticket.assigned`, "You were assigned #123") and from then on gets the ticket's updates. Removing the last riešiteľ hands the updates to every active admin; removing one notifies nobody. Moving the ticket to another client drops the riešitelia who cannot be one there (the old client's clients).
  - There is no ticket history (no activity log) in Spolu yet: the change shows in the ticket page and lists live (`ticket:updated`) and as the riešiteľ's notification.
  - API: `GET /api/tickets/:id/assignee-candidates` (the people who can be picked: every active admin and the group's named active clients, `{ id, name, isAdmin }`), `PUT` and `DELETE /api/tickets/:id/assignees/:userId` (answer the ticket; idempotent). Someone who cannot be one answers `400 invalid_assignee`.
- **Automatic riešitelia** (owner request 2026-11-04, [ADR 0075](../decisions/0075-tickets-assigned-to-managers-finished-by-both-sides.md), `modules/tickets/manager-assign.ts`). A client's account owners (the admins in `group_managers`, active, a substitute's derived rows included; SK "garant") answer its tickets, and nobody configures it:

  | Event | Effect |
  |---|---|
  | New ticket, from any source | Every account owner becomes a riešiteľ, on top of anyone already assigned |
  | The ticket gets a client, or moves to another client | They are added only if it has no riešiteľ left after the move rules |
  | An admin becomes an account owner of the client (`addManager`, a substitution's derived rows; a handler registered with the groups module, in that transaction) | They become a riešiteľ only of the client's open tickets without one |
  | The release (once, in migration 0077) | The rule above for every open ticket, with audit rows and without notifications |

  - Open means New or In progress. Stopping being an account owner changes nothing: a riešiteľ stays until removed by hand. Adding and removing by hand stays.
  - The limit of 10 holds: they are added in the order they became account owners until the ticket has 10 riešitelia; the rest are skipped.
  - Each automatic riešiteľ gets `ticket.assigned`, never about their own action, and an audit row `ticket.assignee_added` authored by the system actor `tickets.manager-autoassign`. A just-assigned account owner gets only that, no `ticket.created` on top.
- **Notifications** (part 21.6, through the **Ticket board** notification group, by default email and push, whatever the group's bell mode; see [notifications](notifications.md)):
  - **who answers a ticket** (simplified 2026-11-04; `modules/tickets/audience.ts`): its riešitelia who can still read it; while there is none (a client without an account owner, no client, or the only riešiteľ was deactivated), **every active admin**, so a ticket never goes dark. A deactivated riešiteľ stays listed until removed by hand. Other clients of the group no longer hear about colleagues' tickets; they still read every ticket on the Tickets tab;
  - a new ticket, or one that gets its client: those people, except the riešitelia just assigned, who hear `ticket.assigned` alone ("New ticket #123 · <client>"; without a client "New ticket #123 without client"); a ticket that keeps riešitelia when it changes its client: its riešitelia ("#123 was changed");
  - a new riešiteľ: that person ("You were assigned #123"); removing one notifies nobody;
  - a chat message (also a thread reply), a status change, a field change, turning into tasks: the people who answer it (above) and its **initiator** ("#123 <subject>", "Jana: text"; "Ticket #123: status “In progress”", "Ticket #123: details updated", "Ticket #123: 2 tasks created"). Resolving is `ticket.resolved` ("Ticket #123 resolved", 2026-10-18, UX review N5h) and reaches the same audience; reopening is an ordinary status change again. The initiator keeps getting the replies and status changes so a requester always hears back (owner decision 2026-10-17); they also get the "Ticket created" email, as before;
  - the lifecycle's own notices: `ticket.awaiting_confirmation` to the author, `ticket.confirmation_declined` to the riešitelia (or every admin while it has none) and the admin who pressed, `ticket.time_review` to an admin (see "Lifecycle" below);
  - someone **named** (@mention) in the ticket chat who reads the ticket gets the mention (owner decision 2026-10-17); one who also hears the message gets one notification; `@all` reaches the people who hear the ticket's updates;
  - only people who can still read the ticket, never the person who made the change; every notification opens `/tickets/<id>`; the email digest has one section per ticket, all in one email per person and window (2026-10-18).
- **Who sees what**:

  | | admin | client of the group | ziadatel of the group |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | tickets of the group | all, also without client | all | only their own |
  | create | for any client | in this group | no |
  | change status, fields, client | yes | no | no |
  | "Solved on our side" | yes | no | no |
  | confirm or decline the mark | no | only the initiator | only the initiator |

- **Lists**: the group's Tickets tab, **My tickets** (the initiator's tickets across their groups) and the admins' **global view** with the client filter (default the clients I manage, "All", "Without client", chosen clients). Filters: status (default Open = New and In progress; Resolved; All), area, riešiteľ (`assignee=me` or `none`; anyone by default). Newest first, 50 per page. Each row carries its riešitelia (two queries for the page, never one per row).
- **Chat.** A ticket with a client has its own chat (part 21.3, see [chat](chat.md)): admins, the group's clients and the initiator write there; it moves with the ticket. A ticket without a client has none until a client is assigned.
- **Solve with Claude** (Phase 29, see [solve-with-claude](solve-with-claude.md)): admins open the ticket in Claude with its fields, text, chat, file names, the instance link and optional notes, to solve it through the eGrant MCP.
- **Navrhnúť odpoveď** (2026-10-19, see [ticket-ai-draft](ticket-ai-draft.md)): in the ticket chat's composer admins get an AI draft of a reply, built from the ticket, its chat, the client's shareable knowledge and similar resolved tickets (another client's identity masked); they insert, edit and send it by hand. Nothing is sent or stored automatically.
- **Turn into tasks** (part 21.5, see [ai-task-split](ai-task-split.md)): admins and the group's clients turn a ticket, or a message of its chat, into task cards on the client's board with the Phase 9 AI split and review dialog. The cards link to the ticket ("Ticket: #123 subject" in the task panel), take its URL as their link when the AI gives none, take its project (else the AI's choice among the client's active projects), keep the web addresses of its text, link its shareable knowledge items and may copy its files and its chat's files; the ticket lists them; the list stays live when such a task is renamed, completed, moved or deleted. A requester sees only "Turned into N tasks", without links, and never the action. A ticket without a client cannot be turned into tasks. The people who hear the ticket's updates get `ticket.tasks` ("#123 turned into 2 tasks").

## Ticket text with AI, voice and knowledge (Phase 33 part P5)

- The text field of the new-ticket dialog and of an admin's edit is the description editor once the ticket has a client (AI button, microphone, "Attach from the knowledge base"); without a client it stays a plain textarea. The text is shown with the chat renderer (Markdown, line breaks kept).
- **Translate the description** (2026-10-19, [ADR 0061](../decisions/0061-translate-received-messages-stored-per-language.md)): on every ticket (since 2026-10-21 also on `.sk` clients and on a ticket without a client, [ADR 0063](../decisions/0063-translate-received-messages-in-every-client-group.md)), an admin or the group's client who did not write the text (its creator, else its initiator) gets **Translate** under it; the translation replaces the text with "Translated from <language>" and **Show original**, as in the chat (`POST /api/tickets/:id/translate`, stored per language until the ticket is edited). The subject is not translated. The ticket chat's messages translate as in every chat.
- Attached knowledge shows as cards under the text for everyone who reads the ticket, the žiadateľ included, who opens the items read-only through the ticket (`GET /api/tickets/:id/knowledge/:itemId`) and downloads their files. A ticket moved to another client shows its old attachments as "Item unavailable". See [projects.md](projects.md#description-editor-part-p5).
- **In the knowledge base** (2026-10-22): the ticket's own files (the form's or the intake's) are listed in its client's Knowledge as read-only "From ticket" items (sk "Z tiketu"), its chat's files as "From chat"; see [knowledge-base](knowledge-base.md#files-from-tickets-and-tasks-2026-10-22-built).

## Project and event (Phase 33 part P2)

- The ticket page's details list has **Project** and **Event** for admins and the group's clients: two selects that save at once (their own route, so clients still cannot edit the other fields). Changing the project clears the event. A žiadateľ sees the project's name only; a ticket without a client shows neither. The new-ticket dialog offers both once a client is chosen.
- List rows show the project chip next to the area. The group's Tickets tab gets the **Project** and **Event** filter pills (in the URL, remembered); the admins' global view shows the Project pill only while exactly one client is chosen. Moving a ticket to another client clears its project and event. See [projects](projects.md#links-and-filters-part-p2).

## Screens (part 21.4)

- **Where.** Admins: "Tickets" in the header (after Tasks) opens the global view `/tickets`, with a count of the tickets with a change I have not seen; the admin dashboard's **Tickets** card lists the active tickets I am a riešiteľ of and those without an active riešiteľ (see [admin-home](admin-home.md)); every group has a **Tickets** tab (Chat, Tasks, Tickets, Knowledge, Members). Clients: the group's Tickets tab; a client who also reported tickets for another client gets "My tickets" in the header. A requester with no client membership lands on **My tickets** (`/tickets`) and the header shows only "Tickets" and the user menu.
- **Lists** (one component for the three places): a row per ticket with the number, the subject (or the first words of the text), the initiator, the area, on My tickets and the global view the client ("Without client" for none), the due date (red when overdue and not resolved), up to three overlapping avatars of the riešitelia ("+n" for more), and the status. Filters: status (Open by default, Resolved, All), area (the areas seen in the list) and riešiteľ ("Assignee: Anyone / Me / Nobody", a menu pill); an old `following` parameter in the URL is dropped; the global view adds the client filter of the global board (default the clients I manage) and "Without client". Filters live in the URL and come back on the next visit (per person, and per client on a Tickets tab; see non-functional.md, "Lists remember their filters"); "Clear filters" appears while any filter is off its default. Rows change live: an edited ticket's row is replaced in place, a new ticket refetches the list, a moved one leaves the old client's tab.
- **Unseen changes** (2026-10-17, [spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-unseen-changes-design.md)): a ticket I got a notification about (created, status, details, riešiteľ, message, tasks, a mention in its chat or, since 2026-10-18, in its text, a time request) after I last opened its page has an unseen change. Its row in the lists and on the dashboard's Tickets card has a bold subject and (2026-10-18) a yellow marker in the row's left gutter: a ring with a dot inside when the changes include a message or a mention ("Nová správa"), else a plain dot ("Nová zmena"). Hover, focus, a tap or a long press shows what changed, from my own notifications ("Nový tiket", "Stav: Vyriešený", "Upravené údaje", "Priradené vám", "1 nová správa (Jana)", "2 nové úlohy z tiketu"; at most 5 lines), from `GET /api/tickets/:id/unseen-summary` (404 to whoever cannot read the ticket now). The ticket page fetches the same summary first, shows it in a small yellow banner under the header (until dismissed) and then marks the ticket seen. Since 2026-10-19 ([ADR 0060](../decisions/0060-read-notifications-when-the-subject-opens.md)) the plain dot also marks a ticket I have an unread notification about (a 👎 rating, for example), and opening the ticket's page by any route also reads all my notifications about it: the bell's count drops and the marks go, live (`notifications:read`).
- **Ticket page** `/tickets/<id>` (all three lists open it): the number, the status and the client over the subject; from 1024 px the details on the left (text, initiator with email and phone, area, the client ("Without client" for none), due date, link, eGrant instance, files, created and resolved times, the riešitelia (for admins and the group's clients the task form's people field: every admin and the group's clients are offered, each pick or removed chip saves at once; for a requester the names only; the hint says a riešiteľ gets the ticket's updates), and the ticket's tasks: for admins and the group's clients a "Tasks" section with a link per task (a completed one struck through) and **Turn into tasks** (while the AI is on; nothing shows without tasks and AI), for a requester only "Turned into N tasks") and the ticket chat on the right; on a phone the details fold above the chat. A **Back** link over the header returns to the list the person came from with its filters (one step back in the app's history), or, opened from outside the app, to their list (admins the global view, a client of the group its Tickets tab, anyone else My tickets). Admins change the status from the header and edit the client and the content in place ("Edit"): the client is a field of that form (a ticket with tasks keeps its client, the select says why), and Save moves the client first with the rules of **Assign or move the client**, then saves the content. A ticket without a client shows "No client yet" (admins choose one in Edit) and no chat. A ticket the viewer cannot read answers the not-found page.
- **eGrant projects** (Phase 33 part P3; rules in [projects](projects.md#egrant-projects-part-p3)): the intake's `project_id` places the ticket in its client's project of that eGrant id, never on an event, once; a ticket whose project has not arrived waits for it, and a ticket without a client is matched when the client is assigned or the ticket moves (a move leaves the old project and event behind). For admins and the group's clients the ticket page shows, over the fields, "eGrant project prj-208 has not arrived yet", the raw "eGrant project: …" on a ticket without a client, "This ticket's project has changes from eGrant. **Review**" (opens the project sheet in Prehľad) and "This ticket's project is archived."; the edit form says under the client select that the project and event stay behind and the eGrant id is matched again. List rows and the admin home's Tickets rows carry the badge "eGrant change" while the project has pending changes, and the lists of admins and the group's clients have the pill **eGrant changes** (`?egrant=1`, server `egrant=pending`). A žiadateľ sees none of it.
- **Time on a ticket** (Phase 32, part A2; rules in `finance.md`, "Time on done", and [Lifecycle](#lifecycle-finished-by-both-sides-2026-11-04)): an admin riešiteľ who presses "Solved on our side", or sets the status to Vyriešený, gets the time dialog (the answer says `askTime`), with the choice "the hours on the linked tasks cover it" when the ticket's tasks hold logged time; the other admin riešitelia are asked by `ticket.time_missing` and a dashboard row. The ticket page shows **My time** under the riešitelia for an admin riešiteľ (everyone's for a finance admin); a client is never asked. A ticket without a client asks too; its time has no client until the ticket gets one (owner decision 24).
- **New ticket** (the group tab and, for admins, the global view): a dialog with the content fields and uploaded files. Admins choose the client (fixed from a group's tab) and name the initiator by email, name and phone ("They get an email and can follow the ticket in the Help app."). A manager (on their group's tab) picks the requester: **Me** (the default; they are the initiator) or **Someone else** (2026-10-23), which shows the same email, name and phone fields with the same hint; the email is then required. After creating, the ticket's page opens.
- **Files in the ticket chat.** Everyone who writes in a ticket chat can attach files; they upload through the ticket (`POST /api/tickets/:id/files`, into the client's files), so the requester, who cannot upload to the group, can attach too. Files uploaded with the form or sent to the intake are readable by whoever reads the ticket, also the requester, and move with the ticket.
- **Requesters** on the Members tab (see `groups.md`) and the task panel's line "Ticket: #123 subject" linking to the ticket, for tasks made from a ticket.
- **Ticket chat messages** of admins and clients have "Turn into tasks" in the message menu and "Turned into tasks:" links under them, like the group chat; the cards link both the message and the ticket. A requester gets neither, and the server sends them the chat's messages without task links.

## Lifecycle: finished by both sides (2026-11-04)

The statuses stay New, In progress and Resolved ([ADR 0075](../decisions/0075-tickets-assigned-to-managers-finished-by-both-sides.md), [spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-ticket-lifecycle-rework-design.md)). The **confirmable author** is the people who can rate the ticket (`canConfirmTicket` is `canRateTicket`): its initiator, with an active account, who is no admin and reads it. On a ticket created on behalf of someone, that is the named person, not the manager who created it.

- **New to In progress.** Opening the ticket page sets `in_progress`, through the page's `POST /api/tickets/:id/seen`, never a `GET`, so MCP reads never count. It needs a ticket with a client, an admin who is its riešiteľ (or any admin while it has no active riešiteľ) and who did not create it (`startsTicketOnOpen`). The update is conditional on `status = 'new'`, so two people opening it at once change it once. It is an ordinary status change with the actor's name: an audit row, a socket event, a notification to the author.
- **The mark.** `tickets.admin_done_at` and `admin_done_by` exist only while the status is `in_progress`; any status change, and moving the ticket to another client, clears them in the same statement. Lists show the badge **Awaiting confirmation** ("Čaká na potvrdenie") instead of the status, and so does the ticket page's header for whoever does not change the status. The Open filter and every open-ticket query stay as they are.
- **The actions:**

  | Who | Action | Route | When | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Any admin | "Solved on our side" (SK "Vyriešené z našej strany") | `POST /api/tickets/:id/admin-done` | `in_progress`, or `new` with a client; no mark | With a confirmable author: sets the mark and notifies them; a new ticket becomes `in_progress` in the same write (one audit row with both changes, `ticket.status` to the other riešitelia, the author hears only the question). Without one: resolves at once, from `new` too |
  | Confirmable author | "Solved" or "Yes, solved" (SK "Vyriešené", "Áno, vyriešené") | `POST /api/tickets/:id/confirm` | `new` or `in_progress`, mark or not | Resolved, the rating opens |
  | Confirmable author | "Not solved yet" (SK "Ešte nie je vyriešené") | `POST /api/tickets/:id/decline` | The mark is set | Clears the mark, notifies the riešitelia (or every admin while it has none) and the admin who pressed |
  | Confirmable author | Writes in the ticket chat (thread replies too) | the chat's write | The mark is set | Clears the mark. A message of thanks does too, on purpose: Help does not guess what a message means, and the admin presses the button again |
  | System | Job `tickets.auto-resolve` | | The mark is 5 calendar days old (`TICKET_CONFIRM_DAYS`) | Resolved as the system actor, `ticket.resolved` to the whole audience, the rating opens |

  A state the action does not fit answers `409 invalid_ticket_state`, and so does a `PATCH /api/tickets/:id` that loses a status race: another write changed the status first, and none of the patch is applied. The admin's status control stays: an admin may set Resolved directly (duplicates, spam) and reopen. A resolved ticket does not reopen by itself: an author's message changes nothing.
- **The job** (`modules/tickets/jobs.ts`) runs hourly at :15, Bratislava time. Each ticket has its own transaction: the row is locked with `for update skip locked`, the mark is checked again on the locked row, so an author's click at the same moment wins cleanly, and one ticket that fails is logged and left for the next run without stopping the others. It pages by keyset on `(admin_done_at, id)` through the partial index `tickets_admin_done_idx`, so tickets that fail or stay locked never hide the ones behind them. The handler throws until the app has attached its deps and sockets (`attachTicketJobs`), so an early run fails loudly and the next hour takes the tickets.
- **Time** moves to the moment the admin finished; rules in `finance.md`, "Time on done".

  | Situation | What the admin sees |
  |---|---|
  | First press of "Solved on our side" | The ordinary time dialog (the answer says `askTime`) |
  | Author confirms, or the job resolves, with the mark still set | Nothing more |
  | The mark was cleared, then the admin presses again | The dialog prefilled with their minutes, with "No change" (SK "Bez zmeny") |
  | The mark was cleared, then the author resolves with no second press | `ticket.time_review` and a dashboard row, opening the same dialog |
  | The author resolves before any press | `ticket.time_missing`, as for a riešiteľ who is not the actor |
  | An admin resolves directly after a clearing | The prefilled dialog, as for a second press |
  | An admin resolves directly with the mark set | Asked as usual, as on any resolve; nobody else is told |

  `admin_done_cleared_at` records that a mark ended and was not set again; a mark ended by a status change or by moving the ticket counts, and so does leaving `resolved` (a reopen), so a later finish offers the second look.
- **Screens.** One bar above the chat, next to the rating card, not folded into the details on a phone (`ticket-confirmation.tsx`): an admin gets "Solved on our side" on a ticket in progress or a new one with a client (a new ticket without a client has no button), and on a marked ticket the line "Waiting for confirmation until <date>"; the author gets "Solved" on a new or running ticket, and on a marked ticket the card "The eGrant team considers the request solved." with "Yes, solved", "Not solved yet" and a hint about the automatic end. Other clients of the group see no action. On a resolved ticket the rating card stays alone. A refused action shows "The ticket has changed in the meantime." and refetches.

## Rating (2026-10-19)

After a ticket is resolved, its **initiator** rates it **Pomohlo** or **Nepomohlo** (👍/👎, thumb icons) with an optional plain-text comment of up to 1 000 characters ([spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-ticket-rating-design.md)). Only the initiator rates, while they read the ticket and are not an admin; other clients of the group, riešitelia and admins never do (an admin initiator does not either). The rating can be set and changed as often as they like for **14 days after `resolved_at`**; after that it is read-only. A reopened ticket keeps its rating and nobody can change it while it is open; resolving it again opens a new 14-day window. No rating changes the ticket's status and nothing is measured against a deadline.

- **The card** on the ticket page, full width under the header and the unseen banner (on a phone it is not folded into the details): "Ako sme vám pomohli?" with the two buttons; a click saves at once (value only) and opens the comment field ("Chcete nám niečo odkázať? (nepovinné)", the hint "Komentár uvidí iba tím eGrant.", a counter from 900 characters, Odoslať / Zrušiť or Esc). Rated: "Ďakujeme za hodnotenie.", the chosen button pressed, the comment, "Hodnotenie môžete zmeniť do <date>." and **Upraviť**. After the window: "Vaše hodnotenie: Pomohlo" and the comment, no buttons. A refused save says "Nepodarilo sa uložiť. Skúste to znova."; a window that closed while the page was open says "Hodnotenie sa už nedá zmeniť." and refetches the ticket.
- **From the notification:** the `ticket.resolved` push and digest open `/tickets/<id>?rate=1`; the page drops the parameter and, for someone who may rate, scrolls the card into view and focuses its first button. The link writes nothing (mail scanners open links): rating needs a click in a signed-in session. There is no separate rating email.
- **Admins** (every admin, owner decision 2026-10-19) see a **Hodnotenie** row in the details after "Vyriešený": each rating with the thumb, the rater's name, the date and the comment (line breaks kept, never Markdown), or "Zatiaľ bez hodnotenia" on a resolved ticket; it updates live. A 👎 notifies the ticket's admin riešitelia once per rating, or every active admin who reads it when it has no admin riešiteľ (`ticket.rated`, see [notifications.md](notifications.md)). The dashboard's Tickets card has a 30-day satisfaction line (see [admin-home.md](admin-home.md)), and the global view links to **Hodnotenia** (`/tickets/ratings`): period pills (30 days, 90 days, 12 months), a client select (all clients by default, the same numbers for every admin), value pills (all, Pomohlo, Nepomohlo), four numbers (Pomohlo, Nepomohlo, Spokojnosť = up / (up + down), "–" with none, and Odozva: tickets resolved in the period that have a rating out of those resolved with an initiator account) and the ratings newest first (by their last change), 50 per page with "Zobraziť ďalšie": thumb, "#123 Subject" linking to the ticket, client, rater, date and the comment clamped to three lines (a click expands it).
- **Privacy:** every admin, and only admins, see ratings and comments; the rater sees their own. Other clients of the group, client riešitelia included, see no value, no comment and no total. A comment is never in a notification, push, digest, socket event, log line or eGrant call, and the MCP layer shows it only through `get_ticket` to an admin's token. The comment may hold personal data: it lives as long as the ticket and the rater's account and goes with them. Anti-abuse: one rating per ticket and person, 20 writes a minute per user, the length limit in the API and the database.

## History of changes (audit log, 2026-10-19)

The audit log „História zmien“ ([audit-log.md](audit-log.md)) records every ticket created (in the app by the person, through the intake as **eGrant** with the instance; a duplicate `request_id` records nothing), edited (status, subject, area, due date, link; the text only as „zmenený“, never the text), moved to a client (`client` from and to), its project and event set, and each riešiteľ added or removed (`assignee_added`/`assignee_removed`, the person in `assignees`). A requester's ziadatel membership made by a new ticket is a `membership.added` row with the same author. Old rows with the programme stay readable. The ticket page's „…“ menu has „História“ for admins.

A **rating** is its own kind of record, „Hodnotenia tiketov“ (`ticket_rating`, under the ticket's id and label, linking to the ticket): the first rating is `created`, a later change `updated`, with the rater as author, in the same transaction as the save. The thumbs are kept from and to; the comment is free text, so only that it was added or changed („zmenený“), never the comment. A save that changes neither records nothing.
