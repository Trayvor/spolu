# Technical support (tickets)

Support tickets come from eGrant's API, from clients and from admins. Each ticket belongs to one client (group), found by the client's eGrant instance link, or to no client yet. A ticket has its own chat, riešitelia (assignees) who answer it, and can be turned into tasks with AI. A person who reported a ticket but is not a client of that group gets a narrow **ziadatel** (requester) membership that sees only their own tickets.

Design: [Phase 21 spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-phase-21-tickets-and-requesters-design.md). Phase 21 supersedes Phases 12 and 13.

## Requirements

- **Sources.**
  - eGrant sends a ticket to `POST /api/tickets/intake` with one shared Bearer key (`TICKETS_API_KEY`); the field list is in [integrations](integrations.md). Every field is optional, but a ticket needs a subject, a body or an area. An optional `request_id` makes retries safe: a repeat answers the first ticket and creates nothing.
  - A client creates a ticket in one of their groups and is its initiator.
  - An admin creates a ticket for any client and names the initiator (email, name, phone), as eGrant does.
  - A ziadatel never creates tickets.
- **Fields**: subject, question area, grant programme, text, due date, a URL, the instance link as received, the initiator's name, email and phone, the number `#123` (one sequence for all tickets), status, source (`api` or `manual`), who created it, when it was created, changed and resolved. A ticket carries files: uploaded with the new-ticket form, or sent by eGrant as base64 in the intake's `files` (up to 10, stored in the client's files; types sniffed from the bytes, see [integrations](integrations.md)). A ticket without a client keeps the intake's files outside any group (only admins read them) until a client is assigned; then they move to it. (The external `file_links` of the first version were removed: eGrant sends the files themselves.)
- **Client by instance link.** The instance link is compared with the groups' eGrant URLs by host: scheme, a leading `www.`, the path and the case do not matter (`https://WWW.X.egrant.sk/app` matches `https://x.egrant.sk`). No match, or no link: the ticket is saved **without a client**. Only admins see it (global view, "Without client"); an admin assigns a client by hand. Until then there is no requester account and no email.
- **Initiator** (for a ticket with a client, by its email; an existing account is found by the exact address, else by its mailbox: case, spaces and a `+tag` never matter, nor, for Gmail, the dots and `googlemail.com`, preferring an admin; so an admin's address in another spelling never makes a second account):
  - an admin's email: that admin;
  - a client of the group: that client;
  - anyone else (a client of another group, a ziadatel of another client, a new person): gets a **ziadatel** membership of this group; a new person gets an account first (named from the ticket, Slovak);
  - someone an admin removed from this group, or a disabled account: no account is linked, only the name and email are kept, no email is sent.
- **"Ticket created" email.** The initiator gets one email per ticket (in their language) with the number, the subject, the first lines of the text and **Open the ticket**. For a žiadateľ (an initiator who is no admin and no client of any group) the button is a one-click sign-in link ([ADR 0022](../decisions/0022-one-click-ticket-sign-in-link.md)): it signs them in without a code and opens `/tickets/<id>` (after asking for their name if it is empty); it works for 7 days, as often as needed, and the email says so and asks not to forward it. An expired link opens the login page with "The link has expired, sign in with your email" and the email filled in. An admin's or a group client's button goes through the code login (`/login?redirect=/tickets/<id>&email=...`), and their email says a code will be sent. It is sent whatever the notification settings, never twice (not on a later move), and not when the initiator created the ticket themselves.
- **Statuses**: New, In progress, Resolved. Only admins change the status and the fields; resolving records the time, reopening clears it.
- **Assign or move the client** (admins). The initiator is found again for the new client and the "Ticket created" email goes if it never went. Riešitelia who cannot be one in the new client are dropped (below). A ticket with tasks cannot move (`409 has_tasks`): its tasks live on that client's board.
- **No per-ticket following** (owner decision 2026-10-17, [ADR 0021](../decisions/0021-ticket-riesitelia-and-watching.md) amendment). There is no Follow button, no followers list and no "Following" filter: who hears about a ticket follows from its riešitelia and from "Watch tickets" (below). The `ticket_followers` table was dropped (migration 0033).
- **Riešitelia (assignees).** A ticket has zero or more riešitelia (EN "Assignees", at most 10), like a task's assignees.
  - Who adds and removes them: admins and the clients of the ticket's group; on a ticket without a client admins only. A ziadatel never (not even on their own ticket).
  - Who can be one: any active admin, and any client of the ticket's group, also one who does not watch its tickets. Never a ziadatel, never a client of another group; a ticket without a client takes admins only.
  - A new riešiteľ is notified (`ticket.assigned`, "You were assigned #123") and from then on gets the ticket's updates instead of the watchers. Removing the last riešiteľ hands the updates back to the watchers; removing one notifies nobody. Moving the ticket to another client drops the riešitelia who cannot be one there (the old client's clients).
  - There is no ticket history (no activity log) in Spolu yet: the change shows in the ticket page and lists live (`ticket:updated`) and as the riešiteľ's notification.
  - API: `GET /api/tickets/:id/assignee-candidates` (the people who can be picked: every active admin and the group's named active clients, `{ id, name, isAdmin }`), `PUT` and `DELETE /api/tickets/:id/assignees/:userId` (answer the ticket; idempotent). Someone who cannot be one answers `400 invalid_assignee`.
- **Watch tickets** (labelled "New tickets of this client: notify me", SK "Nové tikety tohto klienta: upozorňovať", since 2026-10-18, UX review X8; it was "Sledovať tikety"), per user and client group. On, the user hears about every new ticket of that client (`ticket.created`, also one moved to or given that client) and gets **all updates of a ticket while it has no riešiteľ** (chat messages, status and field changes, turned into tasks); the ticket is on their dashboard while it has none. Once a ticket has a riešiteľ, its updates go only to its riešitelia (and its initiator). Off, they hear only about the tickets they are a riešiteľ of, initiated, or are named in.
  - Admins and the group's clients have it; a ziadatel-only person keeps their own tickets only and never sees it.
  - Default: on for the group's clients; off for admins, except that a group's manager has it on while they manage the group (no stored row: the default is computed, so becoming manager turns it on and stopping turns it off). A choice the user makes is stored and kept whatever later happens to managing (the same idea as the manager's notification mode with origin "manager" in `client-managers.md`). So the existing managers and clients started with it on, without a data migration.
  - The group's notification mode (All messages, Mentions and replies, Off) does not change it.
  - API: `GET` and `PUT /api/groups/:id/ticket-watch` (`{ watching }`), for admins and the group's clients (`403` for a ziadatel, `404` outside the group). Stored in `ticket_watch_settings` (`modules/tickets/watch.ts`).
- **Notifications** (part 21.6, through the **Ticket board** notification group, by default email and push, whatever the group's bell mode; see [notifications](notifications.md)):
  - **who answers a ticket** (owner decision 2026-10-17; `modules/tickets/audience.ts`): its riešitelia once it has at least one; while it has none, the people who watch its client's tickets, or **every admin** for a ticket without a client (owner decision 2026-10-17, as every admin sees those on the dashboard);
  - a new ticket, or one that gets or changes its client, while it has no riešiteľ: the people who watch the client's tickets ("New ticket #123 · <client>"); a ticket without a client: every admin ("New ticket #123 without client"); a ticket that already has riešitelia when it gets or changes its client: its riešitelia ("#123 was changed");
  - a new riešiteľ: that person ("You were assigned #123"); removing one notifies nobody;
  - a chat message (also a thread reply), a status change, a field change, turning into tasks: the people who answer it (above) and its **initiator** ("#123 <subject>", "Jana: text"; "Ticket #123: status “In progress”", "Ticket #123: details updated", "Ticket #123: 2 tasks created"). Resolving is `ticket.resolved` ("Ticket #123 resolved", 2026-10-18, UX review N5h) and reaches the people who answer it, its initiator **and the people who watch its client's tickets** even while it has riešitelia, so a client who followed a ticket hears the outcome; reopening is an ordinary status change again. The initiator keeps getting the replies and status changes so a requester always hears back (owner decision 2026-10-17); they also get the "Ticket created" email, as before;
  - someone **named** (@mention) in the ticket chat who reads the ticket gets the mention (owner decision 2026-10-17); one who also hears the message gets one notification; `@all` reaches the people who hear the ticket's updates;
  - only people who can still read the ticket, never the person who made the change; every notification opens `/tickets/<id>`; the email digest has one section per ticket, all in one email per person and window (2026-10-18).
- **Who sees what**:

  | | admin | client of the group | ziadatel of the group |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | tickets of the group | all, also without client | all | only their own |
  | create | for any client | in this group | no |
  | change status, fields, client | yes | no | no |

- **Lists**: the group's Tickets tab, **My tickets** (the initiator's tickets across their groups) and the admins' **global view** with the client filter (default the clients I manage, "All", "Without client", chosen clients). Filters: status (default Open = New and In progress; Resolved; All), area, riešiteľ (`assignee=me` or `none`; anyone by default). Newest first, 50 per page. Each row carries its riešitelia (two queries for the page, never one per row).
- **Chat.** A ticket with a client has its own chat (part 21.3, see [chat](chat.md)): admins, the group's clients and the initiator write there; it moves with the ticket. A ticket without a client has none until a client is assigned.
- **Solve with Claude** (Phase 29, see [solve-with-claude](solve-with-claude.md)): admins open the ticket in Claude with its fields, text, chat, file names, the instance link and optional notes, to solve it through the eGrant MCP.
- **Navrhnúť odpoveď** (2026-10-19, see [ticket-ai-draft](ticket-ai-draft.md)): in the ticket chat's composer admins get an AI draft of a reply, built from the ticket, its chat, the client's shareable knowledge and similar resolved tickets (another client's identity masked); they insert, edit and send it by hand. Nothing is sent or stored automatically.
- **Turn into tasks** (part 21.5, see [ai-task-split](ai-task-split.md)): admins and the group's clients turn a ticket, or a message of its chat, into task cards on the client's board with the Phase 9 AI split and review dialog. The cards link to the ticket ("Ticket: #123 subject" in the task panel), take its URL as their link when the AI gives none, keep the web addresses of its text, link its shareable knowledge items and may copy its files and its chat's files; the ticket lists them; the list stays live when such a task is renamed, completed, moved or deleted. A requester sees only "Turned into N tasks", without links, and never the action. A ticket without a client cannot be turned into tasks. The people who hear the ticket's updates get `ticket.tasks` ("#123 turned into 2 tasks").

## Ticket text with AI, voice and knowledge (Phase 33 part P5)

- The text field of the new-ticket dialog and of an admin's edit is the description editor once the ticket has a client (AI button, microphone, "Attach from the knowledge base"); without a client it stays a plain textarea. The text is shown with the chat renderer (Markdown, line breaks kept).
- **Translate the description** (2026-10-19, [ADR 0061](../decisions/0061-translate-received-messages-stored-per-language.md)): on a ticket of a client whose eGrant link is not on `.sk`, an admin or the group's client who did not write the text (its creator, else its initiator) gets **Translate** under it; the translation replaces the text with "Translated from <language>" and **Show original**, as in the chat (`POST /api/tickets/:id/translate`, stored per language until the ticket is edited). The subject is not translated. The ticket chat's messages translate as in every chat.
- Attached knowledge shows as cards under the text for everyone who reads the ticket, the žiadateľ included, who opens the items read-only through the ticket (`GET /api/tickets/:id/knowledge/:itemId`) and downloads their files. A ticket moved to another client shows its old attachments as "Item unavailable". See [projects.md](projects.md#description-editor-part-p5).

## Project and event (Phase 33 part P2)

- The ticket page's details list has **Project** and **Event** for admins and the group's clients: two selects that save at once (their own route, so clients still cannot edit the other fields). Changing the project clears the event. A žiadateľ sees the project's name only; a ticket without a client shows neither. The new-ticket dialog offers both once a client is chosen.
- List rows show the project chip next to the area. The group's Tickets tab gets the **Project** and **Event** filter pills (in the URL, remembered); the admins' global view shows the Project pill only while exactly one client is chosen. Moving a ticket to another client clears its project and event. See [projects](projects.md#links-and-filters-part-p2).

## Screens (part 21.4)

- **Where.** Admins: "Tickets" in the header (after Tasks) opens the global view `/tickets`, with a count of the active tickets I am a riešiteľ of, those without a riešiteľ of the clients I watch and those without a client; the admin dashboard's **Tickets** card lists the same tickets (see [admin-home](admin-home.md)); every group has a **Tickets** tab (Chat, Tasks, Tickets, Knowledge, Members). Clients: the group's Tickets tab; a client who also reported tickets for another client gets "My tickets" in the header. A requester with no client membership lands on **My tickets** (`/tickets`) and the header shows only "Tickets" and the user menu.
- **Lists** (one component for the three places): a row per ticket with the number, the subject (or the first words of the text), the initiator, the area, on My tickets and the global view the client ("Without client" for none), the due date (red when overdue and not resolved), up to three overlapping avatars of the riešitelia ("+n" for more), and the status. Filters: status (Open by default, Resolved, All), area (the areas seen in the list) and riešiteľ ("Assignee: Anyone / Me / Nobody", a menu pill); an old `following` parameter in the URL is dropped; the global view adds the client filter of the global board (default the clients I manage) and "Without client". Filters live in the URL and come back on the next visit (per person, and per client on a Tickets tab; see non-functional.md, "Lists remember their filters"); "Clear filters" appears while any filter is off its default. Rows change live: an edited ticket's row is replaced in place, a new ticket refetches the list, a moved one leaves the old client's tab.
- **Unseen changes** (2026-10-17, [spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-unseen-changes-design.md)): a ticket I got a notification about (created, status, details, riešiteľ, message, tasks, a mention in its chat or, since 2026-10-18, in its text, a time request) after I last opened its page has an unseen change. Its row in the lists and on the dashboard's Tickets card has a bold subject and (2026-10-18) a yellow marker in the row's left gutter: a ring with a dot inside when the changes include a message or a mention ("Nová správa"), else a plain dot ("Nová zmena"). Hover, focus, a tap or a long press shows what changed, from my own notifications ("Nový tiket", "Stav: Vyriešený", "Upravené údaje", "Priradené vám", "1 nová správa (Jana)", "2 nové úlohy z tiketu"; at most 5 lines), from `GET /api/tickets/:id/unseen-summary` (404 to whoever cannot read the ticket now). The ticket page fetches the same summary first, shows it in a small yellow banner under the header (until dismissed) and then marks the ticket seen. Since 2026-10-19 ([ADR 0060](../decisions/0060-read-notifications-when-the-subject-opens.md)) the plain dot also marks a ticket I have an unread notification about (a 👎 rating, for example), and opening the ticket's page by any route also reads all my notifications about it: the bell's count drops and the marks go, live (`notifications:read`).
- **Ticket page** `/tickets/<id>` (all three lists open it): the number, the status and the client over the subject; from 1024 px the details on the left (text, initiator with email and phone, area, the client ("Without client" for none), programme, due date, link, eGrant instance, files, created and resolved times, the riešitelia (for admins and the group's clients the task form's people field: every admin and the group's clients are offered, each pick or removed chip saves at once; for a requester the names only; the hint says a riešiteľ gets the ticket's updates), and the ticket's tasks: for admins and the group's clients a "Tasks" section with a link per task (a completed one struck through) and **Turn into tasks** (while the AI is on; nothing shows without tasks and AI), for a requester only "Turned into N tasks") and the ticket chat on the right; on a phone the details fold above the chat. A **Back** link over the header returns to the list the person came from with its filters (one step back in the app's history), or, opened from outside the app, to their list (admins the global view, a client of the group its Tickets tab, anyone else My tickets). Admins change the status from the header and edit the client and the content in place ("Edit"): the client is a field of that form (a ticket with tasks keeps its client, the select says why), and Save moves the client first with the rules of **Assign or move the client**, then saves the content. A ticket without a client shows "No client yet" (admins choose one in Edit) and no chat. A ticket the viewer cannot read answers the not-found page.
- **eGrant projects** (Phase 33 part P3; rules in [projects](projects.md#egrant-projects-part-p3)): the intake's `project_id` places the ticket in its client's project of that eGrant id, never on an event, once; a ticket whose project has not arrived waits for it, and a ticket without a client is matched when the client is assigned or the ticket moves (a move leaves the old project and event behind). For admins and the group's clients the ticket page shows, over the fields, "eGrant project prj-208 has not arrived yet", the raw "eGrant project: …" on a ticket without a client, "This ticket's project has changes from eGrant. **Review**" (opens the project sheet in Prehľad) and "This ticket's project is archived."; the edit form says under the client select that the project and event stay behind and the eGrant id is matched again. List rows and the admin home's Tickets rows carry the badge "eGrant change" while the project has pending changes, and the lists of admins and the group's clients have the pill **eGrant changes** (`?egrant=1`, server `egrant=pending`). A žiadateľ sees none of it.
- **Time on a resolved ticket** (Phase 32, part A2; rules in `finance.md`, "Time on done"): an admin riešiteľ who sets the status to Vyriešený gets the time dialog (the `PATCH` answer says `askTime`), with the choice "the hours on the linked tasks cover it" when the ticket's tasks hold logged time; the other admin riešitelia are asked by `ticket.time_missing` and a dashboard row. The ticket page shows **My time** under the riešitelia for an admin riešiteľ (everyone's for a finance admin); a client watcher is never asked. A ticket without a client asks too; its time has no client until the ticket gets one (owner decision 24).
- **Watch tickets** on the group's Tickets tab, next to "New ticket", for admins and the group's clients: a checkbox "New tickets of this client: notify me" with one line saying what it does now ("You get a notification about every new ticket of this client, and its updates until someone is assigned." / "You get notifications only about the tickets you are assigned to or named in."); it saves at once. The same switch is the "New tickets" column of the client table on `/settings/notifications`.
- **New ticket** (the group tab and, for admins, the global view): a dialog with the content fields and uploaded files. Admins choose the client (fixed from a group's tab) and name the initiator by email, name and phone ("They get an email and can follow the ticket in the Help app."); a client is the initiator. After creating, the ticket's page opens.
- **Files in the ticket chat.** Everyone who writes in a ticket chat can attach files; they upload through the ticket (`POST /api/tickets/:id/files`, into the client's files), so the requester, who cannot upload to the group, can attach too. Files uploaded with the form or sent to the intake are readable by whoever reads the ticket, also the requester, and move with the ticket.
- **Requesters** on the Members tab (see `groups.md`) and the task panel's line "Ticket: #123 subject" linking to the ticket, for tasks made from a ticket.
- **Ticket chat messages** of admins and clients have "Turn into tasks" in the message menu and "Turned into tasks:" links under them, like the group chat; the cards link both the message and the ticket. A requester gets neither, and the server sends them the chat's messages without task links.

## Rating (2026-10-19)

After a ticket is resolved, its **initiator** rates it **Pomohlo** or **Nepomohlo** (👍/👎, thumb icons) with an optional plain-text comment of up to 1 000 characters ([spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-ticket-rating-design.md)). Only the initiator rates, while they read the ticket and are not an admin; other clients of the group, riešitelia and admins never do (an admin initiator does not either). The rating can be set and changed as often as they like for **14 days after `resolved_at`**; after that it is read-only. A reopened ticket keeps its rating and nobody can change it while it is open; resolving it again opens a new 14-day window. No rating changes the ticket's status and nothing is measured against a deadline.

- **The card** on the ticket page, full width under the header and the unseen banner (on a phone it is not folded into the details): "Ako sme vám pomohli?" with the two buttons; a click saves at once (value only) and opens the comment field ("Chcete nám niečo odkázať? (nepovinné)", the hint "Komentár uvidí iba tím eGrant.", a counter from 900 characters, Odoslať / Zrušiť or Esc). Rated: "Ďakujeme za hodnotenie.", the chosen button pressed, the comment, "Hodnotenie môžete zmeniť do <date>." and **Upraviť**. After the window: "Vaše hodnotenie: Pomohlo" and the comment, no buttons. A refused save says "Nepodarilo sa uložiť. Skúste to znova."; a window that closed while the page was open says "Hodnotenie sa už nedá zmeniť." and refetches the ticket.
- **From the notification:** the `ticket.resolved` push and digest open `/tickets/<id>?rate=1`; the page drops the parameter and, for someone who may rate, scrolls the card into view and focuses its first button. The link writes nothing (mail scanners open links): rating needs a click in a signed-in session. There is no separate rating email.
- **Admins** (every admin, owner decision 2026-10-19) see a **Hodnotenie** row in the details after "Vyriešený": each rating with the thumb, the rater's name, the date and the comment (line breaks kept, never Markdown), or "Zatiaľ bez hodnotenia" on a resolved ticket; it updates live. A 👎 notifies the ticket's admin owners once per rating (`ticket.rated`, see [notifications.md](notifications.md)). The dashboard's Tickets card has a 30-day satisfaction line (see [admin-home.md](admin-home.md)), and the global view links to **Hodnotenia** (`/tickets/ratings`): period pills (30 days, 90 days, 12 months), a client select (all clients by default, the same numbers for every admin), value pills (all, Pomohlo, Nepomohlo), four numbers (Pomohlo, Nepomohlo, Spokojnosť = up / (up + down), "–" with none, and Odozva: tickets resolved in the period that have a rating out of those resolved with an initiator account) and the ratings newest first (by their last change), 50 per page with "Zobraziť ďalšie": thumb, "#123 Subject" linking to the ticket, client, rater, date and the comment clamped to three lines (a click expands it).
- **Privacy:** every admin, and only admins, see ratings and comments; the rater sees their own. Other clients of the group, client riešitelia included, see no value, no comment and no total. A comment is never in a notification, push, digest, socket event, log line or eGrant call, and the MCP layer shows it only through `get_ticket` to an admin's token. The comment may hold personal data: it lives as long as the ticket and the rater's account and goes with them. Anti-abuse: one rating per ticket and person, 20 writes a minute per user, the length limit in the API and the database.

## History of changes (audit log, 2026-10-19)

The audit log „História zmien“ ([audit-log.md](audit-log.md)) records every ticket created (in the app by the person, through the intake as **eGrant** with the instance; a duplicate `request_id` records nothing), edited (status, subject, area, programme, due date, link; the text only as „zmenený“, never the text), moved to a client (`client` from and to), its project and event set, and each riešiteľ added or removed (`assignee_added`/`assignee_removed`, the person in `assignees`). A requester's ziadatel membership made by a new ticket is a `membership.added` row with the same author. Watching a ticket is personal and not recorded. The ticket page's „…“ menu has „História“ for admins.

A **rating** is its own kind of record, „Hodnotenia tiketov“ (`ticket_rating`, under the ticket's id and label, linking to the ticket): the first rating is `created`, a later change `updated`, with the rater as author, in the same transaction as the save. The thumbs are kept from and to; the comment is free text, so only that it was added or changed („zmenený“), never the comment. A save that changes neither records nothing.
