# Glossary

Terms used across the docs and code, in alphabetical order.

- **@all**: a mention of everyone in the group (shown as "Everyone in this group" in the picker, stored as `<@all>`). It marks the message for every person in the group, like a mention of each of them.
- **admin**: an eGrant team member. Sees all groups and can manage them. Created by the superadmin.
- **admin group**: the one internal group "eGrant tím" (Phase 34), a `groups` row with `kind = 'admin'`. Every admin reads it, no client ever does; it has chat, tasks and knowledge but no tickets, members or managers. Shown first as "Internal" in the admin's client list.
- **ADR**: architecture decision record, a short file in `docs/decisions/` that records one decision and why it was made.
- **API key**: the shared secret (`TICKETS_API_KEY`) eGrant sends as `Authorization: Bearer` with each ticket to the intake API, and with each call of the managers, project and groups webhooks unless the server gives them their own keys (`EGRANT_MANAGERS_API_KEY`, `EGRANT_PROJECTS_API_KEY`, `EGRANT_GROUPS_API_KEY`).
- **Signing secret**: an optional per-webhook secret (`EGRANT_<NAME>_SIGNING_SECRET`) with which eGrant signs each call, `X-Spolu-Signature` = HMAC-SHA256 over `"<X-Spolu-Timestamp>.<body>"`, on top of the API key ([ADR 0042](decisions/0042-egrant-webhook-hmac-signing.md)).
- **AppPrompt**: the one card at the bottom of every signed-in screen that asks to install the app on a phone, then to turn on notifications on this device, or tells an old iPhone that only email works (`<AppPrompt>`, `features/notifications/app-prompt.tsx`). "Not now" hides it for 6 hours on that device.
- **assigned to me**: the open tasks (not in Completed) a person is one of the assignees of. Its total is shown on the dashboard's My tasks card only; badges (the Úlohy tab, the admin sidebar, the header) count unseen changes instead, in black.
- **automatic reminders**: the task board's built-in reminders (Phase 6): every assignee of an open task is reminded 3, 2 and 1 days before its deadline at 09:00, and every 3 hours while it is urgent. They need no setup and cannot be turned off; compare custom reminders.
- **finance admin**: an admin with the finance right (Phase 32), granted only by the superadmin; the superadmin always has it. Sees the Financie tab, `/finance`, finance files and the Finance notification group. A right, not a role.
- **finance item**: a contract, document or note of one client in its Finance tab (`finance_items`, Phase 32 part B); a **contract** carries a number, an amount with its currency and a period, and may have reminders for finance admins.
- **expense** (sk "výdavok", Finance, Phase 32 E): money the organisation pays out, entered on `/finance` only: an admin's salary (names the admin) or another expense (says what it is for), with an amount and a date; dated later than today it is planned. Never in a client's tab.
- **contract of several clients** (Finance, Phase 32 E): a contract one payer, the **parent organisation** (sk "Nadradená organizácia"), pays for several clients at once; each client has a **share** of its amount, and the shares add up exactly to it. A client's tab shows its own share only.
- **organisation item** (Finance, Phase 32 E): a finance item or folder of no client (`group_id` null), shown on `/finance` only.
- **finance-only file**: a file uploaded through Finance (`files.finance_only`); only finance admins read it and no other module can attach or copy it.
- **time entry** (Finance): the minutes one admin spent on one task, one ticket, or a manual entry of a finance admin; counted on its Bratislava day (`finance_time_entries`).
- **time request**: a time entry asked for and not answered yet (minutes empty); the admin sees a "Record your time" row (sk "Zapíšte strávený čas") until they answer, 0 included.
- **covered by the linked tasks** (Finance): a ticket's time answer saying the hours already logged on the tasks made from it cover the ticket; a filled entry of 0 minutes, so nothing is counted twice.
- **hourly rate**: a finance admin's price of one admin's hour from a day on (EUR); the first rate of an admin applies "from the start".
- **project** (sk "Projekt", Phase 33): a client's piece of work with a name, description, files and dated events; tasks and tickets can sit on one. Shown on the group's Prehľad tab. See `product/projects.md`.
- **event** (sk "Udalosť", Phase 33): a dated step of a project (one day, no time); a task or ticket on a project may also sit on one of its events.
- **alias** (projects): an earlier name of a project, kept as a name key so eGrant and new projects with that name still match it; removable in the project dialog ("Also matches").
- **change from eGrant** (sk "Zmena z eGrantu", Phase 33 part P3): one field of an eGrant project delivery that differs from the project in Help (a name, a description, an event's name, date or description, a new event, an event missing in eGrant). It waits in the project sheet until an admin or the client accepts or dismisses it; never applied silently.
- **waiting ticket** (Phase 33 part P3): a ticket whose intake named an eGrant `project_id` that its client does not have yet; it joins the project when the project call delivers it.
- **merge** (projects): moving everything of one project (events, tasks, tickets, files, names, eGrant ids) into another project of the same client and deleting the first.
- **name key**: a name trimmed, spaces collapsed, lower-cased and without diacritics (`normalizeSearch`); one project per name key and client.
- **Prehľad** (EN "Overview", Phase 33): the first tab of a group: the timeline, the open tickets and tasks cards, and the projects. The only screen called Prehľad (see `design/wording.md`).
- **timeline** (Phase 33 part P4): the Prehľad card that shows a client's events in date order on one line with a Today marker.
- **bucket**: a column on a task board. Each group has four fixed buckets (To do, eGrant review, client review, Done; sk Na spracovanie, Kontroluje eGrant, Kontroluje <client>, Hotové) and admins can add custom buckets and reorder every bucket, fixed ones included. Cards can be dragged between and inside buckets.
- **bulk email** ("Hromadné e-maily"): an email an admin writes from blocks and sends to many people in Spolu at once (Phase 23); one email is a **campaign** (`bulk_campaigns`: draft, sending, sent or cancelled). Only bulk email can be unsubscribed from; login codes and notifications always arrive.
- **audience** (bulk email): who a campaign goes to, stored as a filter (`BulkAudience`: client groups, managers' groups, roles, people added or left out by hand); disabled and unsubscribed people are left out. It is frozen into the recipient list (`bulk_recipients`) when sending starts.
- **campaign**: see bulk email.
- **channel**: a place where messages are posted. In Spolu: a group chat, a task chat or a ticket chat (`channels.kind`); direct message chats come with Phase 7.
- **custom reminders**: reminder times someone sets on a task themselves (Phase 19), for themselves, the people they add and anyone who subscribes. They sit beside the automatic reminders, do not move with the deadline, and export to a calendar with the deadline.
- **client**: a person from a customer organization. Sees only their own groups (one or several, Phase 21); in the database the role of their membership in each group.
- **contract**: a record of an agreement with a client (dates, amount, files, notes). Admin-only.
- **dashboard**: the admin home at `/` (Phase 20): a greeting and four count cards (Tickets, My tasks (formerly Assigned to me), Upcoming reminders, Mentions), each a big count beside a list of items that open where they live. On phones the cards are count tiles above the client list.
- **deactivated admin**: an admin who was removed. The row is kept with `disabled_at` set; the person cannot log in, gets no codes and loses open sessions and sockets. Adding the same email again reactivates them.
- **draft contract**: the prepared next contract for a client, with the same fields as a contract.
- **eGrant manager** (of an organisation): eGrant's term for the client's own person. The eGrant managers webhook (`PUT /api/egrant/managers`) makes such a person a **client member** of the instance's group (creating a client account if needed) or removes them; it is not a Help **manager**.
- **egrant URL**: the address of a client's eGrant instance, saved on the group. Incoming tickets are matched to a group by it. It is normalized first (case, protocol, trailing slash and similar differences are removed) so that equal addresses compare equal.
- **email digest**: the batched notification email: one per user and group, sent 10 minutes after the first email-eligible event (10 s in e2e via `EMAIL_BATCH_DELAY_MS`) and only for the messages still unread then, e.g. "3 new messages in <group>" with up to 5 lines. Sent only for the notification groups the user set to Email or Email and push.
- **global board**: the admins' task board across clients at `/tasks` (Phase 20): columns by step (To do, one column per custom bucket name of the chosen clients, at most 12 with the rest in Other steps, eGrant review, Client review, Completed when shown), filtered by default to the clients the admin manages. Cards open the task panel over it; there is no drag and drop.
- **group**: one client organization with its own chat, task board, tickets and contracts.
- **group mode**: what one user hears about one group: All messages, Mentions and replies, or Off. Set with the bell in the group header or on the groups page; without a choice it follows the user's "All groups" default, else the role default (clients All messages, admins Mentions and replies).
- **Help / Help by eGrant**: the product name users see since 2026-10-16 (header wordmark, page title, PWA, emails, push), at https://help.egrant.sk. In running text Slovak says "aplikácia Help" (declined: "v aplikácii Help", "do aplikácie Help"), English "the Help app"; "Help by eGrant" is the full brand. Code name: Spolu.
- **initiator** (of a ticket): the person a ticket is for: the requester eGrant names by email, the client who created it, or the person an admin named. Linked to an account only for a ticket with a client; gets the "Ticket created" email and follows the ticket.
- **invited member** (sk "Pozvaný", Phase 22b): a person an admin added by email on the Members tab who has not signed in yet: an active `client` membership of a user with `email_verified` false. The admin can resend the invitation or cancel it; after the first sign-in they are an ordinary member.
- **invite link**: a link an admin copies from a group. A person opens it, enters an email, gets a code and joins that group when they sign in with it (Phase 21: also a client of another group, or a requester of this one).
- **knowledge folder**: a folder in a group's Knowledge tab (Phase 24); folders nest, and an admins-only folder hides itself and everything inside from clients. Search ignores folders and shows each result's folder path.
- **knowledge item**: a card in a group's Knowledge tab: a note, a file, a link or a reminder, or a chat file ("From chat", sk "Z chatu", Phase 25): a file attached to a chat message, added automatically and read-only. Public (everyone in the group) or admins-only ("Only eGrant"); a client's items are always public. Admins also see all items across clients in the global knowledge list.
- **login code**: the 6-digit one-time code sent by email to log in; valid 5 minutes, 3 tries. Also called OTP code.
- **Návrh odpovede** ("Navrhnúť odpoveď", AI draft of a ticket reply): an admin's AI-drafted reply in a ticket chat, built from the ticket, its chat, the client's shareable knowledge and similar resolved tickets (another client's identity masked); inserted into the composer for review, never sent or stored automatically. See [product/ticket-ai-draft.md](product/ticket-ai-draft.md).
- **MCP (Model Context Protocol)**: the open protocol AI assistants (Claude Code, Claude Desktop) use to call tools. Help's MCP server is at `/mcp`; an admin connects an assistant with a personal **MCP token** (`spolu_mcp_…`, made on `/settings`, read only or read and change), or adds Help in Claude as a **custom connector** with just the URL (OAuth: Claude registers itself and the admin approves it on the consent page, a **connection**), and the assistant then acts as that admin ([integrations/mcp.md](integrations/mcp.md), ADR 0034).
- **manager**: an admin who manages a group (an eGrant account manager for that client). A per-group flag, not a role: a group can have several managers and an admin can manage several groups; each admin becomes or stops being one only for themselves. Not the same as an **eGrant manager**. A disabled admin does not count. Becoming manager turns the group's mode to All messages unless the admin chose one.
- **member**: a user who belongs to a group: a `group_members` row with a role (`client` or `ziadatel`); removed by an admin when `removed_at` is set.
- **mention**: tagging a user with @ in a message so they are notified.
- **attached work**: a task or ticket of the channel's group attached to a chat message with the attachment picker (Phase 34); shown as a live card, "unavailable" once deleted or moved to another client.
- **message**: one post in a channel. Can have attachments, a thread and mentions.
- **module**: a self-contained feature area of the server (for example chat or tasks) that owns its routes, policies and tables.
- **notification group**: one of chat, task board, ticket board, knowledge base: every notification event belongs to one, and the user picks its channel (Email, Push, Email and push, None) in the profile. Keys `chat`, `tasks`, `tickets`, `knowledge`. Not the same as a client group's notification mode (bell), which decides what arrives.
- **notification event**: one stored notification for one recipient (kind, optional group, payload, delivered or not). Recorded in the same transaction as the change that caused it and deleted after 30 days.
- **notification centre**: the list behind the bell in the header (2026-10-18): the person's notification events of the last 30 days that delivery judged worth telling them about (`in_centre`), with "Mark all as read" and the push snooze ("Stíšiť"). See `product/notifications.md`.
- **OTP code**: the one-time code sent by email that is used to log in.
- **owner walkthrough**: the final check of a phase, where the owner tries the "Done when" items by hand on the test server after the phase is merged; what is still to walk is noted in the phase's roadmap section.
- **passkey**: a WebAuthn credential an admin keeps on a device or in a password manager and logs in with instead of the email code (ADR 0050); shown as "kľúč (passkey)" in Slovak. Only admins have them.
- **pending client**: a client who entered an email on an invite link but never signed in (`email_verified` false, empty name). A later invite link replaces their pending invite; once they sign in they are a normal client of that group.
- **pending invite**: the invite a person typed their email on, kept until they sign in with a code (`pending_invites`, Phase 21); the latest one wins.
- **pickable people**: who can be picked as an assignee in a group: its named, verified, active clients and its active managers. Every other admin is a reader of the group (can be mentioned and read it) but is not pickable. See `listPickablePeople`.
- **policy function**: a server function that decides whether a user may see or change a resource; written test-first.
- **push subscription**: one browser's permission to receive Web Push for one user, stored per device in `push_subscriptions` (endpoint and keys). Created after the browser grants permission, removed at logout or when the push service answers 404/410.
- **PWA**: progressive web app. The web app can be installed on a phone or desktop and receive push notifications.
- **quick switcher** (command palette, "Hľadať"): the global search opened with Cmd/Ctrl+K or the header's magnifier button: groups, tickets, tasks, knowledge, projects and (admins) people the person may read, plus quick actions ([product/search.md](product/search.md)).
- **read state**: what a user has already read in a channel, and in each thread they started or replied in; used for the unread indicator.
- **recipient (of a reminder)**: someone a reminder notifies: its author (always), people the author added, and people who subscribed themselves. Recipients get it in every group mode, Off included.
- **reminder**: a scheduled notification. A knowledge reminder (Phase 17) and a task's custom reminders (Phase 19) notify their recipients at the chosen times; a task's automatic reminders (Phase 6) remind its assignees of the deadline and urgency; a contract reminder (Phase 15) tells admins that a contract is about to need attention.
- **reminder set**: the times and recipients of one subject (a knowledge reminder item or a task), kept by the reminders module; its author is the knowledge item's author, or the person who added a task's first custom time. Each time fires once, by the every-minute `reminders.fire` job, as a `reminder.due` notification to every recipient.
- **removed member**: a client an admin removed from a group. The user row is kept with `disabled_at` set and the membership stays, so the admin can restore them; they get no code and lose sessions and sockets. Listed under the group's removed members.
- **REDIRECT_DOMAINS**: the optional deploy variable listing old domains (space-separated, e.g. `spolu.egrant.sk`) that answer a permanent redirect to the current `DOMAIN`; see [runbooks/deploy.md](runbooks/deploy.md), "Moving to a new domain".
- **request_id**: eGrant's own id of one intake request (1 to 100 characters). Spolu keeps one ticket per id, so a retry with the same id answers the first ticket (`200`) and creates nothing; see [integrations/egrant-ticket-intake.md](integrations/egrant-ticket-intake.md).
- **room**: a Socket.IO room; users join rooms to receive live events for a group, DM or task channel.
- **saved reply** (code: snippet, `reply_snippets`; F7): an admin's canned reply, private or shared with every admin, inserted with `/` in a composer; `{{name}}` in it becomes the name of the person answered. SK "uložená odpoveď".
- **saved view** (`saved_views`; F5): a person's named filter set of the global board, the tickets, the knowledge or the finance page, stored as the page's URL and shown as a pill under its filter row. SK "uložené zobrazenie".
- **seq**: a per-channel increasing number on each message. Clients use it to find and load messages missed during a disconnect.
- **shareable with clients**: the admin group's word for a `public` knowledge item (Phase 34 P34-C); only such an item can be attached in a client's chat or description, where that client opens it read-only. Its opposite there is "Only eGrant" (`admins`), the default.
- **session**: a long-lived login on one device, kept after the code is entered. A `session` row plus the httpOnly cookie `better-auth.session_token`; removing a user deletes their sessions.
- **Vyriešiť s Claude** (solve with Claude, Phase 29): the admins' action on a ticket or task that prepares a prompt with its context and optional notes and opens it in the admin's own claude.ai, to solve it through the eGrant MCP connector; Spolu runs no AI for it. See [solve-with-claude](product/solve-with-claude.md).
- **Spolu**: the code name (Slovak for "together") used in code, package names (`@spolu/*`), databases, Docker and these docs. It was the visible name ("Spolu by eGrant") until 2026-10-16, when it became Help. Formerly the working title eHub.
- **Stretnutie** (meeting): a knowledge item for a meeting or call (Phase 30): date and time, links, notes, transcript, a compact audio copy of the recording, and an AI summary with decisions, action items and open questions. Teams T1 can give it a **Teams meeting** (created from Help or connected by its link), whose transcript Help imports and holds for admins until shared.
- **Séria stretnutí** (meeting series, recurring call): a call that repeats (weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly on the date or weekday); each occurrence is a Stretnutie of its own, created up to 60 days ahead ([ADR 0049](decisions/0049-recurring-calls-rule-and-materialised-occurrences.md)).
- **subscribe (to a reminder)**: "Remind me" on a reminder: anyone who can read the item adds themselves as a recipient, and can leave again. The author cannot leave their own reminder.
- **superadmin**: the first admin, created automatically at start from `SUPERADMIN_EMAIL` (name from `SUPERADMIN_NAME`). Cannot be deleted or demoted; creates other admins.
- **task**: a card on the task board with title, description, one or more assignees, deadline, an urgent flag and attachments.
- **task chat**: the discussion inside one task, with threads, mentions and attachments.
- **Teams organizers group** ("Help – Teams organizers"): the Microsoft 365 security group whose members' calendars and meetings Help may use; the organizer of a Teams meeting made from Help must be in it ([integrations/teams.md](integrations/teams.md)).
- **test environment**: the Oracle Cloud VM where Spolu is deployed, at https://help.egrant.sk; `main` is deployed there with `scripts/deploy-local.sh` (since 2026-10-18; before, every merge deployed it); environment name `test`.
- **thread**: replies grouped under one top-level message (the thread root); a reply cannot start another thread. Opens in a side panel, linked by `?thread=<rootId>&c=<channelId>`.
- **ticket**: a support request (from eGrant's API, a client or an admin) with a number `#123` and a status (New / In progress / Resolved), shown in its client's Tickets tab, in My tickets and in the admins' global view; without a client only in the global view.
- **ticket follower**: removed 2026-10-17 (no per-ticket following). A ticket's updates go to its riešitelia, or to the people who watch its client's tickets while it has none (every admin without a client), and to its initiator.
- **riešiteľ** (plural riešitelia; EN UI "assignee"): a person who handles a ticket: an admin or a client of the ticket's group. A ticket has zero or more; while it has any, its updates go to them instead of the watchers.
- **watch tickets** (SK "Sledovať tikety"): a per-user, per-client-group switch; on, the user is notified about every new ticket of that client and about all updates of a ticket while it has no riešiteľ (owner decision 2026-10-17). On by default for clients and the group's managers.
- **ziadatel** (requester): a person who reported a ticket but is not a client of that group (Phase 21). Their membership has the role `ziadatel`: they see only their own tickets of the group, never its chat, tasks, knowledge or members. One person can be a ziadatel of several clients.
- **unsubscribe** (bulk email): a person's choice to get no more bulk email, made through the email's footer link, the mail app's one-click `List-Unsubscribe`, or the "Receive mailings" checkbox in Settings (which also subscribes again). Stored in `bulk_subscriptions` (no row = subscribed); it never affects login codes, invites, notifications or ticket emails.
- **unsubscribe token**: the signed part of a bulk email's unsubscribe link, `<userId>.<campaignId>.<signature>` (HMAC-SHA256 with `APP_SECRET`); nothing is stored, and a test email uses the campaign id `test`.
- **urgent**: a flag on a task. An urgent card shows a red "Urgent" pill on the board, and (from Phase 6) its assignees are reminded every 3 hours between 08:00 and 20:00 until it is in Completed or no longer urgent.
